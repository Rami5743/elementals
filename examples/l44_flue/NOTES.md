# Flue (`l44_flue`) — Fire + Earth
**Introduces:** a gale. At fan strength 2.7 Fire rises by itself, and Earth doesn't, but Earth on Fire's head goes up with it. Until now only Air could lift anyone.
1. **Solution:**
   1. Earth pushes the boulder onto the heavy plate, which runs the fan for good.
   2. Fire rides the fan up, lands on the ledge and lights the torch, which opens Earth's gate. Fire drops down the gap beside the ledge.
   3. Earth goes through the gate and climbs onto Fire's head. Fire walks into the fan and carries Earth up to the top floor.
   4. Fire melts the ice. Earth holds the heavy plate, which opens Fire's gate. Fire goes through to its door, and Earth walks to its own.
2. **Roles:** Earth places the boulder and holds the top plate. Fire lights the torch, carries Earth and melts the ice.
3. **Why each is necessary:** Earth's yard is walled off, and only the torch opens it. The ice and Fire's gate reach the roof, so neither can be jumped. The torch is 12 tiles up, out of reach of a boost (about 7.4 tiles), so Fire needs the fan, and only something heavy on the plate runs it. Earth can't rise in the fan alone. The boulder has to do the holding, because Earth has to leave the plate and still have the fan. The ice needs Fire, and the top plate needs Earth.
4. **Insight:** Lighter isn't weaker: in a strong enough wind, Fire is the lift.
5. **Failure/recovery:** Nothing here can dead-end. The boulder can be pushed back onto the plate, the torch stays lit, and the gap brings Fire back down. Earth under Fire doesn't work: Fire flies off its head, so Earth has to be on top.
- **Counterfactuals:** Earth idle means no boulder, so no fan and no torch. Fire idle means the torch stays unlit, so Earth stays in its yard.
