# Box Relay (`l05_box_relay`) — Fire + Water
**Introduces:** pushable boxes as steps, boxes crossing liquid.
1. **Solution:** Water wades across the pool, hops over the box and pulls the lever (opens Fire's gate). Water pushes the box back across the pool until it bumps into Fire. Fire hops onto the box and Water pushes box and Fire to the foot of the 3-tile ledge. Fire jumps up and holds the plate that opens Water's hut. Water walks home and Fire walks to its door.
2. **Roles:** Water handles the box and the lever. Fire holds the plate.
3. **Why each is necessary:** Only Water can cross the pool. The plate must be held while Water walks into its hut, and Water can't do both.
4. **Insight:** Boxes ignore liquids, and a plate needs a body at the right moment.
5. **Failure/recovery:** If the box is pushed into the pool, Water can push it back. Climbing the ledge by standing on Water's head also works.
- **Counterfactuals:** Fire idle means nobody holds the plate while Water goes home. Water idle means the lever is never pulled, the box never crosses and Fire's gate stays shut.
