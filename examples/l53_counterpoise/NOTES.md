# Counterpoise (`l53_counterpoise`) — all four
**Introduces:** a seesaw as a ratchet. The right pan rises through a one-way ledge and comes back without what it carried, as a lift does in level 46, but here the lift is the team's own weight: boulder 3 plus crate 1 is 4, and only all four players together weigh 5. A short seesaw (38 px of travel) leaves no gap to fall through, a post stops anyone walking under the right pan, and a bridge over the left pan lets everyone reach the ledge without tipping the scale.
1. **Solution:**
   1. Everyone climbs onto the bridge and drops through it onto the left pan. With all four on, the right pan rises past the ledge.
   2. Everyone gets off. The right pan sinks, and the boulder and the crate stay on the ledge.
   3. Earth climbs up and hops over the crate, between it and the boulder. Fire hops onto the boulder and melts the ice at the mouth of the tunnel.
   4. Water stands on the freeze pad on the bridge, which freezes the strip in the tunnel. Earth pushes the boulder through to the far (heavy) plate.
   5. Air pushes the crate through to the near plate. Water can leave the pad once the crate is across.
   6. With both plates on, the gate opens. Everyone goes home.
2. **Roles:** All four weigh in. Fire melts the ice. Water freezes the strip. Earth pushes the boulder. Air pushes the crate (anyone but Earth, who is at the far end, can).
3. **Why each is necessary:** 4 can't lift 4: it takes all four on the pan, and leaving anyone out only balances the scale. The ice needs Fire. The strip is 4 wide under a 2-tile roof, so Earth can't jump it with the boulder, and only Water's pad freezes it. The heavy plate needs the boulder, because Earth has to go through the gate too. The light plate needs the crate for the same reason: anyone can stand on it, but the last player through can't.
4. **Insight:** It takes everyone to lift what only one of you can push.
5. **Failure/recovery:** Everyone can step off and start the weighing again. The pool stays frozen for 7 s after Water leaves the pad, and Water can step back on. Fire can ride across on the boulder. **This level can dead-end:** pushing the boulder past the far plate up to the gate strands it, and pushing the crate off the right pan onto the left one loses it. Each needs a restart.
- **Counterfactuals:** Any idle player leaves the left pan at 4 at most, so the boulder and crate never rise.
