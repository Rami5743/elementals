# Updraft (`l09_updraft`) — Fire + Water + Air
**Introduces:** fans powered by torches, gliding, riding updrafts.
1. **Solution:** Water holds the freeze pad and Fire crosses the pool and lights the torch, which starts the fan. Air rides the fan to the high ledge and pulls the lever that opens the exit gate, then glides down. Water douses the flames in front of Air's door.
2. **Roles:** as above.
3. **Why each is necessary:** Only Air rises in fans (Fire and Earth are too heavy). The fan only runs with the torch lit. The pool is too wide for Fire. The flames block Air's door.
4. **Insight:** One element's ability switches on another's.
5. **Failure/recovery:** The pool stays frozen for 4 s after Water steps off. Torch and lever latch.
- **Counterfactuals:** Fire idle means no fan. Air idle means the gate stays shut. Water idle means no ice and Air's door stays blocked.
- *Note:* Air could glide Fire across the pool on its head. Water is still required for the fire wall.
