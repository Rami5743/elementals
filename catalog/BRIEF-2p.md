## Idea keys no 2-player level has touched

- `boulder-carry` — Something that falls on a player rides on their head until a low roof scrapes it off.
- `lock-per-element` — One barrier per element, each opened by exactly one of them.
- `two-is-not-heavy` — A heavy plate takes max(mass), not the sum: two light players standing on it together do nothing, so the weight has to be fetched.
- `door-pushes-you-through` — A closing door pushes a player down through a one-way tile — the only way down there, and you cannot do it to yourself.
- `tower-presses-a-column` — Switches stacked up a wall that nothing but a tower of players reaches, with the order of the tower forced by what stands at the bottom. (Campaign: Totem.)
- `lift-sorts-by-weight` — A lift that can be told to stop for heavy loads and carry light ones past, so who gets off where is a setting rather than a choice. (Campaign: Weighbridge.)
- `plank-sums-plate-maxes` — The game's two weighing machines disagree about what a crowd weighs. A heavy plate reads max(mass) and ignores riders, so two light players on it are worth one light player; a balance plank reads the sum of each half, recursively through heads, so two of them weigh exactly what Earth weighs and three outweigh Earth. The same bodies are therefore interchangeable with the stone on the scales and worthless on the lock, which decides which of the two jobs the one heavy player has to do in person. And because inequality gives the same full swing whatever the margin, the plank has only three states, of which 'level' is an exact arithmetic coincidence rather than anything a switch can cause.
- `pound-needs-headroom` — A ground pound is a jump first: measured, Earth standing on a cracked panel with one tile of roof cannot leave the ground at all, so there is never an airborne Down press and the floor is simply unbreakable, while two tiles are enough. A cracked panel in a crawl therefore cannot be opened by the player who owns it, only by whoever can take the ceiling away — and when that ceiling is the floor of the storey above, giving Earth the room to pound means pulling the party's own road out from under them. The barrier is neither the floor nor the roof but the distance between them.
- `fractional-rider-keeper` — Air's half-unit rope weight exactly balances a ballasted pan; Fire's full unit moves it and then leaves to perform a Fire-only task, while Air alone retains the mechanical permission.
- `stone-held-from-below` — A rolling boulder holds 4 px a tick and nobody runs faster than 3.4, so the only thing that ever stops one is a body already standing in its path — and it comes to rest in the cell immediately UPHILL of that body, one row up, never on their head. A heavy plate hung in that cell is therefore an unforgeable key: the body standing under it is too light to press it and cannot climb to it, and the one who pushed the stone can never park it there, because you push from above and hold from below and nobody can be both. The eight ticks a free-rolling stone spends crossing the cell are far too few for anything that has to stay open long enough to walk through. And because the stone rolls on the instant the brake steps aside, and follows the brake downhill, one stone can be walked from a high berth to a lower one but never back up without Earth: gravity fixes the order of the berths, and giving up the high one is the price of the low.

- **Hot & Cold** `t02_hot_and_cold` — Each element wades through its own liquid to a lever that opens someone else's door.
- **Lend a Hand** `t03_lend_a_hand` — A human ladder up the cliff, then an elevator for whoever stayed below.
- **Ice & Flame** `l04_ice_and_flame` — Two tunnels, two barriers. Each of you can only clear the other's way.
- **Box Relay** `l05_box_relay` — A box, a pool and a plate that has to be held at just the right moment.

- **Mudworks** `l13_mudworks` — Earth wades where Fire can't; Fire climbs where Earth can't. Swap favours three times.
- **Crosswind** `l14_crosswind` — Mist for Air, water for Water, sludge for nobody. Air will have to carry a passenger.
- **Bellows** `l15_bellows` — Fire lights the way for Air, Air holds the door for Fire. Back and forth, five times.
- **Riverbed** `l16_riverbed` — Water takes the high road and Earth the low road. Each one keeps the other's road open.
- **Dust Devil** `l17_dust_devil` — Earth is the ladder, the pusher and the hammer. Air is the key and the wind.
- **Foundry** `l23_foundry` — Two crates, two pools, one gate. Every crate is needed twice, in the right order.

- **Ferryman** `l33_ferryman` — A one-seat gondola that crosses while enough switches are on and comes back when they aren't. Some of you start on the wrong side, and so does the crate.
- **Battering Ram** `l34_battering_ram` — Nobody here can push a boulder. The wall can.
- **Hod Carrier** `l35_hod_carrier` — A crate has to cross a gorge. Crates can't fly. Air can, with one on its head.
- **Thermals** `l36_thermals` — This updraft could never lift Fire. But jump into it, and you come down much later.
- **Jack** `l37_jack` — These bridges sink as soon as you let go. Unless something is in the way.
- **Flue** `l44_flue` — A gale strong enough for Fire, and for Fire with a passenger.
- **Leap of Faith** `l45_leap_of_faith` — A crag too high to fly to, and too far to jump to.
- **Dumbwaiter** `l46_dumbwaiter` — A lift can carry a boulder past a ledge. The ledge keeps it.
- **Stamp Mill** `l47_stamp_mill` — A crate can't fall through a grate. It can be hit through one.
- **Long Haul** `l48_long_haul` — One turbine, one boulder, one flight.
- **Portcullis** `l56_portcullis` — Each plate is far from its gate. Somebody has to keep the gate from shutting.

- **Catch the Lift** `l58_catch_the_lift` — The button is a long way from the lift, and the lift doesn't wait.
- **Furnace Line** `l59_furnace_line` — A belt carries the boulder to the furnace. You have about ten seconds.
- **Rockslide** `l66_rockslide` — A boulder that rolls, a plate it rolls past, and a partner in its way.
- **Stepping Stone** `l68_stepping_stone` — Uphill with a boulder, so that it can roll down into the lake.
