## Idea keys no 4-player level has touched

- `lift-press` — A lift used as a jack: it raises an object into a switch hanging in mid-air.
- `lift-corner-path` — A shuttle whose path turns a corner, so it is bridge and lift at once.
- `fan-passenger` — An updraft strong enough only if it carries the right pair.
- `glide-dismount` — Stepping off Air in mid-glide, which reaches a place Air itself cannot.
- `glide-passenger` — Air carries somebody across a gap nobody else can cross.
- `pound-to-move` — Pounding the floor out from under an object instead of under yourself.
- `one-way-drop` — Down drops you through a ledge: a route that only runs one way.
- `wide-door` — A door that opens sideways: a wall that becomes a bridge, or the reverse.
- `dark` — The level is dark; lit torches are the only light, and the map has to be learned.
- `liquid-lane` — Each element wades its own liquid to something the others need.
- `twist-stranding` — Whoever is stranded has to be freed by what their own work opened.
- `twist-sacrifice` — A resource is spent: once used, that route or object is gone for good.
- `twist-self-block` — The thing that helps you is also what is in your way.
- `door-pushes-you-through` — A closing door pushes a player down through a one-way tile — the only way down there, and you cannot do it to yourself.
- `climb-then-steer` — In an updraft you rise only while you do not steer, so leaving at the right floor means giving up the climb at the right moment.
- `partner-builds-your-landing` — The place you are going to does not exist yet: a partner has to put it there while you are already in the air.
- `counterweight-you-must-remove` — A gate held open by the weight of the very thing the rest of the level needs, so going on means shutting it.
- `tower-presses-a-column` — Switches stacked up a wall that nothing but a tower of players reaches, with the order of the tower forced by what stands at the bottom. (Campaign: Totem.)
- `single-file-stone` — A boulder in a corridor one tile high is an absolute wall that only Earth can move and only ever forwards, so the one stretch of raised roof is the only place anybody can get past it: where the stone is parked decides who can be on which side of it.
- `stand-aside-is-the-switch` — In a corridor where nobody can pass anybody, the one place to get out of the walking line is a shelf above it — and that shelf is the switch, so standing aside and holding the far door open are the same act, and whoever does it cannot be the one who goes.
- `unload-the-pan-you-ride` — A seesaw used as the only route between three storeys, one flush with each of its positions. Only the heavier pan is ever at floor level, so the far pan can never be loaded from below: a weight has to be put on your OWN pan to send theirs up to the storey where it can be loaded at all, and that same weight has to be shed again before your pan will carry you anywhere. You rise by what is taken off, never by what is added. (The campaign's scales — Assay, Counterpoise — sum weights too, but to balance a pan under a floating plate or to hoist cargo past a ledge; here the sum decides which storey each of you can reach, and the beam has no switch and no wiring at all.)
- `mass-adds-on-the-beam` — A seesaw compares the SUM of everything on each pan — bodies, cargo, riders standing on other riders — while a heavy plate reads only max(mass), the heaviest single thing on it. The same two bodies are worth 3 to the beam and 2 to the plate; two crates weigh exactly what Earth weighs; and the stone that is merely one side of a sum on the beam is an unforgeable key on the pad. (Campaign: Assay, Counterpoise.)

## Campaign 4-player levels

- **Hot & Cold** `t02_hot_and_cold` — Each element wades through its own liquid to a lever that opens someone else's door.
- **Lend a Hand** `t03_lend_a_hand` — A human ladder up the cliff, then an elevator for whoever stayed below.
- **Four Corners** `l10_four_corners` — Everyone to their own corner. The vault opens only when all four plates are held at once.
- **Windmill** `l11_windmill` — A chain reaction: every element powers the next.
- **Convergence** `l12_convergence` — The finale: crack, drop, burn, blow, freeze and douse. In the right order.
- **Clockwork** `l20_clockwork` — Four buttons in four corners, each behind another element's lock. Press them together.
- **Heartstone** `l22_heartstone` — The grand finale: three chambers, every element needed in each.
- **Bucket Brigade** `l29_bucket_brigade` — Pass one crate through lava, water, mud and mist. Each of you can only carry it through your own.
- **Interlock** `l30_interlock` — Four sealed quarters, four airlocks. Each airlock's switch is in someone else's hands, and so is every way home.
- **Eclipse** `l31_eclipse` — In the dark: a boulder only a two-key lift can reach, a crate that is a stone and then a key, and a vault that needs four keys at once.
- **Elementarium** `l32_elementarium` — The finale. Four wings, each sealed by one element's liquid, each holding a key that its owner can't win alone. Carry a friend in, work together, carry them out.
- **Ferryman** `l33_ferryman` — A one-seat gondola that crosses while enough switches are on and comes back when they aren't. Some of you start on the wrong side, and so does the crate.
- **Keystone** `l42_keystone` — One boulder, passed from hand to hand across the map. This time Earth doesn't touch it.
- **Grand Works** `l43_grand_works` — A bridge that needs a prop, a shaft that needs a thermal, and a seesaw that needs nearly everyone.
- **Counterpoise** `l53_counterpoise` — It takes everyone to lift what only two of them can move.
- **Quorum** `l54_quorum` — Gates that open for the right number, and shut again for too many.
- **Last Word** `l55_last_word` — A gale, a leap, a bridge, and a gate that can count.
- **Portcullis** `l56_portcullis` — Each plate is far from its gate. Somebody has to keep the gate from shutting.
- **Paternoster** `l64_paternoster` — Two lifts, two buttons, each on the wrong side.
- **Last Shift** `l65_last_shift` — A boulder on its way to the furnace, two plates, and a gate that hardly opens.
- **Convoy** `l74_convoy` — Two boulders, two riders, one tunnel of sludge, and the way home for those who stay behind.
- **Avalanche** `l75_avalanche` — The finale. A boulder down a long slope, someone running ahead of it, a gale, and everyone's door in someone else's hands.
- **Weighbridge** `l90_weighbridge` — A lift that weighs its passengers: with the lever on, anything heavy gets off halfway.
