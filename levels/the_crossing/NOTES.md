# The Crossing (`the_crossing`) — Fire + Water + Earth

**Introduces:** a shuttle whose path turns a corner. It runs along the floor of the hall, out over
the sludge, and only then climbs — so it is the bridge and the lift at the same time, and the
berth it waits at is the one tile of floor past which there is nothing.

1. **Solution.** Fire hops up for its gem and melts the ice that shuts the western end. Fire and
   Water move clear and take up the two eastern berths. Earth walks through the melted ice, round
   to the far side of the boulder, and shoves it the length of the western hall onto the heavy
   plate: the shuttle starts its round. Earth comes back to the westernmost berth. The three wait
   for the shuttle to return, step aboard, and ride twelve tiles out over the sludge and then
   seven tiles up. They step off onto the gallery; Water puts out the flames across it and all
   three walk to their doors.

2. **Roles.** Fire opens the western hall; nothing else melts ice. Earth is the only one who can
   move the boulder, and the only mass the plate accepts. Water is the only one who can clear the
   gallery.

3. **Why each is necessary.** Fifteen tiles of sludge separate the hall from the gallery and the
   gallery is seven tiles above the hall floor, so the shuttle is the only route; it only moves
   while the heavy plate is down, and only the boulder can hold it. `check` confirms all three
   counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The shuttle has to be caught, not called: it leaves as soon as the plate goes
   down and comes back on its own, so the three of you have to be standing on its berths before
   Earth lets go of the boulder — or wait a full round for it.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Stepping onto an empty berth drops you into the sludge and
   restarts the level; the berths are the three tiles past the end of the floor, and the shuttle
   pauses three seconds at each end, which is time enough to walk on. The boulder cannot be lost:
   it ends against the western wall on its plate, and pushed too far it only sits on open floor.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The sludge spans every tile between the hall and the
  gallery and nothing can be pushed into it. The gallery is seven tiles up with no ledge below it,
  out of reach of a jump, a head-stand or a three-player tower from the hall floor. The ice reaches
  the roof, and the flames on the gallery do too.

- **Kinship with the campaign.** Campaign levels 26 and 33 use a gondola that runs while switches
  are held, and 58 and 64 use lifts whose buttons are elsewhere. What is not in the campaign is a
  carrier that turns a corner — here the same machine is first the only bridge and then the only
  lift, which is why its berths are over a drop.
