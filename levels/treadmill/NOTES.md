# Treadmill (`treadmill`) — Fire + Water + Earth + Air

## The intended solution

A three-tile belt runs leftward at 2.8 px a tick, under a roof one tile high so
nobody can jump it. Fire and Water run at 3.2 and Air at 3.4, so the three of
them gain 0.4–0.6 px a tick and crawl across in about four seconds. Earth runs
at 2.6. Earth never gets across at all.

1. **Fire** melts the ice at the mouth of the hall.
2. **Water** hops over Fire, puts out the flames behind the ice, and leans into
   the belt first.
3. **Fire**, then **Air**, follow across. Air stops on the turbine, which opens
   the gate in the cellar below.
4. **Earth**, left behind, breaks the cracked floor just short of the belt and
   drops into the cellar. It walks under the treadmill, through the gate Air
   opened, and throws the lever at the far end.
5. The lever opens the vault on the surface. The other three walk through to
   their doors; Earth's own door is at the end of the cellar.

## Why each player is needed

- **Fire** and **Water** clear the ice and the flames: both are three tiles tall
  under rock, so neither can be jumped.
- **Air** is the only one who can spin the turbine, and the cellar gate is the
  only way to the lever.
- **Earth** is the only one who can break the cracked floor and the only one who
  can reach the lever — and the vault that lever opens is the only way to the
  other three doors. The level is a trade: they cross for Earth, Earth opens for
  them.

## The insight

The belt is not an obstacle to be timed or avoided — it is a *speed test*, set
exactly between Earth's legs and everyone else's. The moment you notice Earth
cannot gain a pixel on it, the question stops being "how do we get Earth across"
and becomes "what can Earth do down there that we cannot do up here".

## Likely failures and how players recover

- **Earth tries the belt.** It simply never arrives; it is pushed back to the
  edge. Nothing is lost but time, and the cracked floor is one tile behind it.
- **Someone lets go halfway across.** The belt carries them back to the near
  side. Walk in again.
- **Earth breaks the floor while someone is standing on it.** They drop into the
  cellar with Earth. They can jump back up through the hole — it is three tiles,
  inside everyone's jump.
- **Air leaves the turbine.** The cellar gate is latched; once open it stays open.

## Shortcuts that were closed

- The belt's roof is one tile high, so the three tiles cannot be jumped, by Earth
  or anyone else. Without that roof Earth could hop across in two bounds.
- The cellar's ceiling is solid everywhere except the hole Earth makes, so the
  other three cannot use it to skip the belt: the cellar leads only to the lever
  and to Earth's own door.
- The vault is three tiles tall under rock.

## Kinship with the campaign

The campaign's belts carry loads past the players (Furnace Line, Sorting Yard,
Last Shift, Express). This one carries the players, and its speed is the puzzle.
