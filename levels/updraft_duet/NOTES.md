# Updraft Duet (`updraft_duet`) — Water + Air

**Introduces:** a landing made of ice that starts melting the moment you leave the pad that froze
it. The draught carries a pair, but slowly, and the whole climb is spent inside a countdown that
you started yourself.

## The intended solution

1. **Water** stands on the pad at the far end of the hall. The shelf at the top of the chimney —
   the only floor between the draught and the gallery — freezes over.
2. **Water** leaves the pad, which starts the thaw, walks to Air and climbs onto its head.
3. **Air** carries Water into the draught. With a passenger it climbs slowly; the clock is running
   the whole way up.
4. At the top, Air steers out of the chimney and the pair come down on the frozen shelf. Water
   steps off and both cross onto the solid gallery.
5. **Water** puts out the flames and throws the lever. That stops the draught for good — there is
   no second trip — and opens the hatch at the end of the gallery.
6. **Water** shoves the crate down the hatch. It lands on the plate in the hall below, which opens
   the gate in front of both doors.
7. Both follow the crate down the chute and walk to their doors.

## Why each player is needed

- **Water** is the only one who can freeze the shelf, the only one who can put out the flames, and
  the one who has to leave the pad to be carried — which is what makes the crossing a race.
- **Air** is the only one the draught lifts, and the only lift Water will ever have. Once the lever
  is thrown the draught is dead, so Air's ride is a one-off.

## The insight

Freezing the shelf and being carried to it are the same job done by the same player, and they cannot
be done at the same time. Everything after that follows from how much of the clock the climb eats:
Air alone would be up in a second, Air with a passenger takes most of what Water bought.

## Likely failures and how players recover

- **Climbing aboard too slowly.** The shelf thaws before the pair arrive and they drop back down the
  shaft into the hall. The pad is still there; go round again. A checkpoint sits in the hall.
- **Throwing the lever before the gallery is finished with.** The draught stops and there is no way
  back up. Everything up there — the gem, the crate — has to be done first.
- **Pushing the crate before the lever is thrown.** The hatch is shut and the crate stops on it;
  throw the lever and push again.
- **Standing on the shelf when it thaws.** That is water, and only Water survives it.

## Shortcuts that were closed

- The chimney is three tiles wide with rock on both sides for eleven rows: without the draught
  there is no way up and no way back up.
- The shelf is the only floor between the chimney and the gallery, and it is a pool, so it is only
  there while it is frozen.
- The gate in front of the doors is opened by the plate under the hatch, not by the lever, so the
  crate has to make the trip too.
- The flames are three tiles tall under rock.

## Kinship with the campaign

Updraft and Crosswind both have one element ferrying another up a draught. Neither of them makes the
place you are being carried to something the passenger had to create first, with a clock on it.
