# Updraft Duet (`updraft_duet`) — Water + Air

**Introduces:** carrying a passenger up a fan. The chimney's updraft is the default strength: it
lifts Air easily, Water not at all, and Air with one passenger slowly but surely.

1. **Solution.** Water collects its gem on the hall floor while Air walks clear of the chimney and
   stands still. Water climbs onto Air's head. Air steps sideways into the updraft and the pair
   drift up the fourteen-tile chimney, taking the neutral gem on the way. Near the top Air steers
   out of the draught and lands on the loft floor; Water steps off and walks to the flames that
   cut the loft in two and puts them out. Air takes its gem, walks through to its door, and Water
   takes the near one.

2. **Roles.** Air is the lift. Water is the passenger and the only one who can open the far half
   of the loft.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The chimney is the only way up and its draught is far too weak for
   Water, who would need about twice the strength to rise alone — so Water only gets up there on
   Air's head. The flames run from the loft floor to the roof, and only Water puts them out, so
   Air alone reaches the loft and stops. `check` confirms both counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** A fan does not lift a player, it lifts a stack. Adding Water's weight to Air's
   lift still comes out positive, which is exactly why the level gives Air one passenger and not
   two.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** There is no liquid and nothing deadly in the level: the worst
   that happens is Water slipping off Air's head and dropping back into the hall, from where it
   walks over and climbs on again. The climb is slow, which makes the steering at the top
   unhurried; overshooting the loft simply means drifting back into the draught and trying again.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The chimney's walls are solid rock from row 8 to row 14
  and the loft floor is solid everywhere but the chimney mouth, so there is no ledge to jump to
  and nothing to climb: the hall is fourteen tiles below the loft, four times a jump and twice a
  boost. The flames are two tiles with rock above them to the roof. Nothing in the level can be
  carried, pushed or spent.
