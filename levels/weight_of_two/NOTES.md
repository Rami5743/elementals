# Weight of Two

Batch-90: 59/90

## Core idea
The pulley sums bodies; a heavy plate uses a maximum. Fire and Water together outweigh the crate and lower their platform, but even standing together on the wide heavy contact they cannot hold the west gate. Earth must replace them. Later two separate ordinary plates need both light bodies at once, while Earth repeatedly travels between the heavy contact and the hatch controls. Three distinct notions of weight or attendance therefore govern a single route.

## Solution
1. Fire boards the wide pan; alone it balances the one crate on the other side.
2. Water joins it. The two outweigh the crate, lowering their pan and raising the crate to its contact.
3. The contact latches the entrance to Earth's compartment. Earth walks west to the wide heavy plate.
4. Fire and Water pass west and hold two distinct ordinary plates, latching the east authorization.
5. Earth leaves the heavy plate and walks east to open the descent hatch below both light players.
6. Both light players descend. Earth returns to the heavy plate to permit passage in the lower gallery.
7. Fire and Water walk east and simultaneously occupy the distant pair of ordinary plates. Fire passes the latched far gate and sets the far certificate, opening the final western compartment.
8. Water returns west through the gate held by Earth. Fire follows, reaches the western certificate and authorizes Earth's exit.
9. Earth can finally leave the heavy plate and walk east to the exit.

All three actors are indispensable. Neither light actor alone tips the pulley, and the heavy plate does not sum their masses. Two separate contacts require two bodies. Removing Earth prevents both heavy-controlled passages. The lower gallery is separated by a thick floor, except for the latched hatch; neither light actor can substitute for Earth's contact, and Earth cannot occupy the separated contacts simultaneously.

## Recovery
A solo boarding attempt can wait indefinitely for the second passenger; after one passenger steps off at the lower station, the remaining passenger balances the rope against the crate, so there is no dismount timing requirement. The recovery replay delayed Water by 180 ticks and verified the upper contact did not activate with Fire alone. Another replay deliberately placed both light players on the same heavy plate, verified its signal remained off for 60 ticks, then retreated and completed the level with Earth holding it. The heavy plate is reusable, Earth can always return to it, and every certificate gate and the descent hatch latch. No hazardous tiles or irreversible cargo moves.

## Novelty
Two-is-not-heavy is new at three players. Makeweight (four players) uses a seesaw and a heavy loft plate; here a rope with a one-crate counterweight preserves its height when one passenger dismounts, and the same pair that outweighs that crate must be replaced at a heavy contact and then split across separate ordinary contacts twice.
