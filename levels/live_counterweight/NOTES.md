# Live Counterweight

Batch-90: 86/90

**Introduces:** mass-adds-on-the-beam at three players. A live light rider is the third unit that tips a loaded balance against Earth, and the other light operator must leave the counterweight to certify a remote contact.

## Solution
Earth rolls the stone onto the near pan, rides down, collects the gem and reboards from the cellar. This raises the far pan flush with Fire and Water's gallery. Fire loads two crates onto the far pan and moves clear. Water boards the remaining spot, releasing the chute. Earth pushes the stone off onto the heavy pad: only the combined two crates and Water can now raise Earth. Fire descends through the chute, travels west to the stone-authorized receipt lever, then east to the live contact. The heavy pad, receipt and held contact together open Earth's upper gate. When Earth is home, Water leaves the pan into the lower hall and reaches the eastern exit. Fire can now release the contact and return west to the exit. Four gems are collected.

## Necessity, shortcuts and recovery
Only Earth can shift the stone; Water must supply the third unit while Fire is off the pan, and Fire must activate the lower receipt and hold the live contact while Water remains on the balance. Two crates alone, premature withdrawal of the live rider and skipping the remote lower contact are independently simulated failures. The live rider can step off and reboard while the far pan is still at the gallery; this is tested in the recovery replay. The beam is weight-driven rather than timed, with no lethal terrain. If Water descends prematurely, the gallery chute and the lower hall give access to the beam, and Earth can lower the near pan by adding weight again. Fire must let Water clear the lower pan before returning under it.

## Novelty
Steelyard is a two-player precedent for unloading a boulder to reverse the beam. Its far pan uses three permanent crates. Here one is replaced by a live rider whose continued presence is logically incompatible with operating the remote lower contact: the two light elements must split their responsibilities, and the final release waits until the rider can be withdrawn. The three-player Weight of Two uses a pulley, not a seesaw with a live counterweight and a separately held exit contact.
