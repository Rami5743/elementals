# Launch Pad (`launch_pad`) — Fire + Water

**Introduces:** the two-player boost — jumping off a partner while the partner is jumping.

1. **Solution.** Water hops up for its gem and walks to the mark below the shelf. Fire climbs onto
   Water's head. Water jumps; a moment later Fire jumps off Water's head, takes the neutral gem on
   the way up and lands on the shelf five tiles above the floor. Fire walks to the lever, which
   opens the gate at the far left, collects its gem at the far end of the shelf and goes to its
   door up there. Water walks through the open gate to its own door.

2. **Roles.** Water is the launch pad and the only one who can be down there to use the gate. Fire
   is the one thrown, and the only one who can reach the lever.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The shelf is five tiles above the floor. A single jump reaches 2.8
   tiles and standing on a partner's head adds only one more, so nothing but a boost gets anyone
   up there — and a boost needs two players. `check` confirms it: with Water parked in its door
   nobody can launch Fire, and with Fire parked in its door nobody can reach the lever, so Water's
   gate never opens.

4. **The insight.** Two jumps timed one after the other are worth more than twice one jump. The
   shelf is deliberately put just out of reach of every single-player move.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Jumping too early is the usual miss: the measured window is
   about 6 to 20 ticks after the carrier leaves the ground, roughly a tenth to a third of a
   second, which is wide enough to feel fair. A miss drops Fire back on the open floor with
   nothing lost, and they try again. There is no hazard in the level and nothing that can be
   spent, so it cannot be failed, only retried.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The shelf's left and right faces are both five tiles of
  plain rock, so there is no climbable step anywhere; the only object in the level is the lever on
  top. A head-stand reaches 3.6 tiles, measured, and a boost reaches 6.3, so the five-tile shelf
  separates the two cleanly. Water's gate is four tiles of door with rock from row 15 to the roof
  above it, so it cannot be jumped either.
