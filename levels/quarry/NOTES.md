# Quarry (`quarry`) — Fire + Earth

**Introduces:** the ground pound as a way to move something, not just to break a floor. The
boulder cannot be pushed anywhere, so the floor under it has to go instead.

1. **Solution.** Fire hops up for the neutral gem, walks to the ice in the passage and melts it.
   It climbs the two-tile staircase to the gallery, takes its gem, steps up onto the post, walks
   along the boulder's back and down onto the cracked boards, carries on to the end of the gallery
   and drops to the floor out of the way. Earth wades into the mud for its gem, follows the same
   route and stops on the boards beside the boulder. A jump and a ground pound (Down in mid-air)
   smash the boards: the boulder drops six tiles onto the heavy plate below, the gate opens, and
   Earth walks out to its door with the boulder behind it. Fire follows.

2. **Roles.** Fire opens the passage; nothing else melts ice. Earth brings the boulder down;
   nothing else breaks a cracked floor or weighs enough to matter to the plate.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The plate is heavy: a crate or Fire on it does nothing, and the only
   mass in the level is the boulder and Earth itself — and Earth has to walk through the gate it
   opens. The gallery's cracked boards are the only way the boulder can leave its pen. `check`
   confirms both counterfactuals: with Fire in its door the ice never melts and Earth never gets
   past the passage; with Earth in its door the boards never break and the gate stays shut.

4. **The insight.** A boulder wedged against a post cannot be pushed in any direction — but it is
   standing on something, and Earth can take that away.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Nothing in the level kills except the mud, which is Earth's
   own and safe for it; Fire never has to go near it. The pound takes a little height, so it has
   to be done from a jump rather than a standing press, and a failed attempt costs nothing. Once
   the boulder is on the plate it stays: Earth lands to the right of it and walks away from it, so
   it is never pushed off.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The boulder is pinned between the post at column 22 and
  the cracked boards, and there is no standing room on its left-hand side at all, so it cannot be
  pushed off either end of the gallery and stranded. The ice is two tiles with rock to the roof
  above it and the gate is three, likewise. The staircase is the only route from the left of the
  passage to the right-hand hall, and it runs over the gallery, so the pen cannot be bypassed.
  The roof over the gallery leaves four tiles of headroom, enough for the pound to be comfortable.
