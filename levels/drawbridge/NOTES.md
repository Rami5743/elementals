# Drawbridge (design draft)

**Core:** a weight on the mark eats the drawbridge back from its far end to the mark, so everything
beyond the mark falls and everything short of it stays; only Water survives the fall, so Water has
to be thrown in first to make the fall safe for Earth.

## Plan

- **The keep road.** Upper deck, three tiles of headroom: the gatehouse A (hinge side, west), the
  drawbridge T (a wide door, 22 tiles), the keep B (east) with the three doors behind gate G.
- **The mark.** A heavy plate across T's full headroom, five tiles out from the hinge. While it is
  pressed T opens, which for a wide door means it shrinks from its free (east) end back to its
  hinge at 4 px a tick: faster than anyone runs, so a weight can never cross, and Earth, the only
  heavy player, can never cross either.
- **The flooded hall** under T and the keep: a water pool (Water wades it, Fire and Earth drown),
  Water's freeze pad at the west end, dry floor at the east end with the heavy gate plate HB, a
  one-tile step the stone cannot climb, and a hoist up through a one-way grating into the keep
  (worked by Fire's lever on B; it can only deliver upward, so nobody rides it down).
- **The stone** starts on the bridge just short of the mark.

## Each player's job

- **Earth**: the only one who moves the stone; the only heavy thing besides the stone.
- **Water**: the only one who survives a fall into the hall while it is flooded, and the only one
  who can freeze it.
- **Fire**: the only one who can be in the keep to work the hoist when Earth and Water are below.

## Forced order

1. Fire crosses to the keep (the mark ignores a light player).
2. Water walks out beyond the mark.
3. Earth pushes the stone on to the mark and **backs off at once**: the bridge is eaten from the far
   end; Water goes into the water, the stone floats, Earth must not be beyond the edge when it gets
   back to the mark. The stone falls out of the mark and the bridge grows back.
4. Water wades to the pad and freezes the pool.
5. Earth steps on to the mark and waits for the floor to come and take it: it lands on the ice.
6. Earth pushes the stone across the ice on to HB: gate G opens.
7. Earth and Water hop the stone, climb the step, stand on the hoist; Fire pulls the lever.
8. All three walk through G to their doors.

## Twist and wrong plans

- Twist: Water has to be thrown in by the stone, and Earth has to stand on the very mark that drops
  it.
- Wrong plan 1: Earth pushes the stone across and follows it: the floor goes, Earth drowns.
- Wrong plan 2: Water steps on the mark to go down: nothing happens, it is light.
- Wrong plan 3: Earth goes down before the pool is frozen: drowns.
