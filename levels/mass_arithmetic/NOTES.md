# Mass Arithmetic

Batch-90: 65/90

## Core idea
A single mechanical comparator has three operating regimes: two light riders sink the wide pan, exactly one balances the opposing boulder via the 2:1 snatch block and the pan's own half-unit of ballast, and an empty pan rises. Consequently the party can retain the high contact while sending one partner away, but must eventually unload both riders to transfer the stone's signal to the low contact. This is a handoff between a stored mechanical state and electrical permissions.

## Intended solution
1. Fire boards the wide pan. Its weight plus the platform's half-unit exactly balances the opposing boulder divided by two.
2. Water joins Fire. Their combined weight lowers the pan and raises the boulder to the heavy high contact. No individual light rider can achieve this.
3. Fire dismounts on the lower floor. Water remains aboard; the balance holds the high signal steady while Fire reaches the eastern workshop and sets its far certificate.
4. Water dismounts. The empty pan rises and the stone descends to the heavy low contact. Together with the eastern certificate this opens the western workshop.
5. Water enters the western workshop and activates the permission for the original upper floor. The lower descent hatches remain closed.
6. Water takes the independent recall lift up to the newly authorized top compartment, sets the top certificate to open both hatches, then rides the lift back to the lower workshop. Fire can now descend at the eastern hatch.
7. Water descends via the western hatch. Both players record the bottom-floor keys, then withdraw them to reopen their own inverse approach gates. The two central passage permissions are latched.
8. They hold opposite ordinary plates, jointly certifying the central gate, then exchange sides to collect gems and enter their respective exits.

## Necessity and recovery
The solo-rider attempt fails because the rope remains balanced. The second rider remaining on the pan fails because the low contact is never powered. Both idle-at-exit checks fail. The independent recall lift has generous 300-tick dwell times; no narrow jump or timed input is required. If the riders get separated before a full descent, the shaft and the recall lift allow a return to the upper pan; no hazardous tiles are present. If both dismount prematurely after Fire has entered the eastern workshop, the high gate closes, but the far certificate can reopen it from within the chamber. A dedicated recovery replay deliberately caused that closure before setting the certificate, asserted the low contact and closed gate, and finished successfully. A separate solo-boarding recovery delayed Water and asserted that the high contact did not activate before the second rider arrived; that replay also won. The descent hatches stay closed until their upper certificate has been set. Both irreversible drops are therefore authorized before the party leaves the upper region.

## Novelty
The new idea is three-load-rope-control. Rope Register uses a single rider and externally switched mechanical advantage to write and erase a stable height. Here the changing number of riders supplies all three regimes without changing the rope ratio; both players jointly create the high signal, one maintains it, and the empty pan yields its complement. Weight of Two at three players uses Earth as a separate heavy-plate operator, whereas this two-player version uses the same opposing boulder to alternate between the two distant heavy contacts and an independent return route. The central challenge is recognizing when to change the load, rather than navigating a timed crossing.
