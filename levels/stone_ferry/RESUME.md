# Stone Ferry — resume notes

**Design in two sentences.** A rolling boulder is the only boat across a sludge moat: liquids are
floors to it and riders go with it, so it carries one rider per trip, only downhill, and only Earth
can roll it back (east lift, held by whoever just crossed, then the gallery to the trapdoor). Air's
turbine both opens the trapdoor and *shuts* the slope gate, so Air must stay to drop every stone and
crosses last: spin, let go, run and be aboard before the 200-tick run-down ends.
