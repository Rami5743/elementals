# Quartet (`quartet`) — all four

**Introduces:** four locks and one tower. Each element opens exactly one of the four, and the one
that is not an ability is a three-player boost.

1. **Solution.** Air hops into the turbine at the near end — nothing else reaches it — and the
   first door latches open. Water walks through, takes its gem and puts out the flames beyond.
   Earth takes its own gem and plants itself under the shelf; Water climbs onto Earth and Fire
   onto Water. Earth jumps, Water a moment later, Fire last, and Fire sails nine tiles up onto the
   shelf, taking the neutral gem on the way. Up there Fire melts the ice, takes its gem and pulls
   the lever, which opens the gate at the end of the hall. Fire drops off the near end of the
   shelf, Earth walks through the gate and ground-pounds the cracked floor, and all four drop into
   the vault where the doors are.

2. **Roles.** Air opens the hall, Water opens the passage and is the middle of the tower, Earth is
   the base and the only one who can break the vault floor, Fire rides to the top and works the
   lever.

3. **Why each is necessary.** Four barriers, four elements: a turbine answers only to Air, flames
   only to Water, a cracked floor only to Earth's pound, and the ice on the shelf only to Fire —
   and the shelf itself is nine tiles up, which takes three players jumping in turn. `check`
   confirms all four counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** With four players the question is not who can do a thing but who can afford to
   be where: the tower spends three of them to put the fourth where the lever is.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** The tower's jumps must be staggered, with a window of roughly
   ten to twenty ticks between each pair; a failed tower drops everyone on open floor. The first
   door latches, so it never has to be opened twice. Nothing in the level is deadly and nothing
   can be spent; only the cracked floor is one-way, and the doors are under it.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** All three doors and the ice run from the floor to the
  roof, the turbine is five tiles clear of the door it opens, and the shelf runs from column 20
  into the right-hand wall so there is no way onto it from the far side. The hall holds no crate,
  boulder or ledge that could be climbed.
