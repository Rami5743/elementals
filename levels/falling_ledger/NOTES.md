# Falling Ledger

Batch-90: 74/90

**Introduces:** the same heavy object switches circuits permanently by falling through its floor, changing a stationary permit into a reversible two-position register.

## The intended solution
1. Fire passes the upper-contact gate and authorizes the release.
2. Earth reaches the cracked floor and pounds it, permanently transferring the stone to the lower belt.
3. The upper contact disappears, opening the inverse gate.
4. The stone travels west; Fire records the west certificate.
5. Fire reverses the belt; the east contact combines with the west certificate to authorize Earth.
6. Earth reaches the eastern workshop and records its certificate.
7. Fire reaches the final workshop and records its certificate.
8. Fire reverses the belt again; the west contact completes both exit permissions.
9. Both collect their gems and exit.

## Why each player is needed
Earth alone can break the cracked floor and reach the isolated upper certificate. Fire controls the release and the direction of the lower belt and records the two lower certificates.

## The insight
Do not keep the original permission indefinitely: it must be destroyed to create the new register. The east setting alone is also insufficient; return to west after recording its result.

## Likely failures and how players recover
An early reversal sends the stone east before the western certificate is available. Fire can restore the belt from the same accessible station, then follow the intended route. The recovery simulation checks this. Keeping the stone upstairs leaves the inverse gate closed, but Earth can still break the floor. The belt is reversible and the stone cannot leave its chamber. No precision timing or jumping is needed.

## Shortcuts that were closed
Keeping the upper floor intact and leaving the register at the east end were scripted and do not complete. All gates reach the roof and neither player can enter the other player's workshop.

## Kinship with the campaign
Stamp Mill moves a crate through a grating. Here the broken floor destroys an upper circuit and creates an independent two-position lower circuit. Adapted from Drop Register for a different player count; no two-player level has previously used drop-reconfigures-circuit. Descent Ledger transfers people, not a cargo contact between circuits.
