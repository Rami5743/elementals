# Quartet Imprint

**Introduces:** A jointly powered hoist writes a physical memory onto a grate. Air cannot leave its control room while its power key is active, so the retained cargo state must substitute for its relinquished permission.

## The intended solution

1. Air enables its permit and Fire raises the jointly powered hoist. The stone activates the high contact.
2. Air releases the permit, lowering the hoist and opening its own inverted gate. The grate retains the stone, so Air, Water and Earth can reach their first relays.
3. Fire returns its hoist switch to off. Earth closes the press, erasing the high state. The low contact and three relays latch Fire's first gate.
4. Fire sets its first relay. Earth and Air set their second relays. Earth reopens the press; Air returns to renew the permit.
5. Fire raises the stone. The high contact and three relays enable Water's middle gate. Water sets its relay, closing its own return route while Fire's middle gate latches.
6. Fire sets its next relay. Air releases the permit; Water undoes its relay and returns to the first room. Fire turns off the hoist switch. Earth erases the state.
7. Air reaches its last relay using the low contact and return permissions. Earth sets its last relay, allowing Water to operate the final permission.
8. Air and Fire renew joint power while Earth reopens the press. Fire writes the high state and reaches its last gate. Air releases the permit for the final time. All collect gems and exit.

## Why each player is needed

Fire writes the high state, Earth erases it, Water relinquishes the middle relay and supplies the return permission, and Air supplies the second hoist input and intermediate permissions. All four idle-at-exit tests fail.

## The insight

The high state outlives the power that produced it. Air must turn off the permission that raised the cargo in order to move. The stone stores permission without consuming either operator's attention. Earth uses a different mechanism to erase it, and Water exchanges a live relay for recorded permission.

## Likely failures and recovery

Retaining Air's permit closes its return route; release it. Lowering the hoist alone leaves the stone above the grate; close the press. Retaining Water's middle relay closes its return gate; undo it from the same room after Fire latches its permission. Latches retain prior work and the hoist and press repeatedly rewrite the state. No timed crossings or hazardous gaps. The continuous state space has not been exhaustively searched.

## Shortcuts

Direct walking fails. Retaining Air's permit fails at the inverted gate. Lowering the hoist without using the press fails waiting for the low contact. Rock separates the galleries and isolates the stone; full-height gates prevent jumping around them.

## Kinship with earlier levels

Grate Register and Three-Way Imprint developed reversible cargo memory. Quartet Imprint adds jointly controlled writing and an operator who must relinquish a live input while the cargo retains the state. Unlike Rope Palindrome, the remembered value is not an equilibrium height of balanced pulley masses: it is a stone resting on one-way terrain, and erasing the value specifically requires a separate closing press.
