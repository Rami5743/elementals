# Headroom (`headroom`) — all four

**Introduces:** carrying a boulder on your head and letting a low roof take it off again. Anything
that falls on a player stays there and travels with them; a ceiling one tile above the floor
scrapes it straight off, which makes the mouth of a tunnel a place you can put something down
precisely.

1. **Solution.** Fire melts the ice that shuts the hall, takes its gem and steps back. Air spins
   the turbine at the near end, which latches the inner gate open, and hops up for its own gem.
   Earth jumps onto the ledge, takes the gem up there, and waits. Water stands under the lip of
   the ledge; Earth shoves the boulder off the edge and it lands on Water's head. Water walks east
   with it, wades the six tiles of water nobody else can cross, and walks into the low tunnel on
   the far bank — the roof lifts the boulder off and drops it on the heavy plate at the tunnel's
   mouth. That opens the gate at the far end for Water and the gate at the near end for the other
   three.

2. **Roles.** Fire opens the hall, Air opens the inner gate, Earth is the only one who can move
   the boulder at all, and Water is the only one who can take it across.

3. **Why each is necessary.** A boulder is mass three, so only Earth shifts it; the water is six
   tiles wide, past Fire's three-tile jump and Air's running jump, and lethal to both; the heavy
   plate takes nothing lighter than the boulder; the ice and the turbine are each one element's
   business. `check` confirms all four counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The boulder cannot be pushed across and the one who could push it cannot swim.
   The only way the two facts meet is to put the boulder on the swimmer — and then the only way to
   get it off again, out in the middle of a bank, is to walk somewhere too low to carry it.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Standing in the wrong place when the boulder comes off the
   ledge simply drops it on the floor, where Earth can push it back up — the ledge is two tiles,
   so the boulder can be shoved back on. Water stepping into the water without the boulder costs
   nothing; the boulder, dropped in the water, floats and can be pushed on. The one deadly thing
   is the water itself, for everyone but Water, and it is plainly drawn.

- **Kinship with the campaign.** l63 has Air bring a crate through on its head and l35 has a crate
  flown over a gorge, so carrying is not new; l47 gets a crate through a grate by hitting it. What
  is not in the campaign is taking the load off deliberately, by walking somewhere that has no
  room for it.
