# Ballast Run (`ballast_run`) — Fire + Water

**Introduces:** a heavy plate in a level with nobody heavy in it. A plate's load is the *largest*
mass standing on it, never the sum, so two players on it together weigh exactly as much as one —
which is to say, not enough.

## The intended solution

1. **Water** walks to the flames that block the line and puts them out. They are the first thing in
   the way of the boulder as well as of the players.
2. **Fire** hops over Water, melts the ice further down the line, and keeps walking.
3. **Fire** touches the torch. The torch is wired to the line, so the belt starts and stays started,
   and the boulder that has been sitting at the near end begins its trip.
4. Both players walk off the belt onto the solid floor at the far end — comfortably, since walking
   with the belt is nearly twice the boulder's speed.
5. The boulder rides the length of the hall and comes to rest on the heavy plate, which latches the
   vault open. Both walk to their doors.

## Why each player is needed

- **Water** is the only one who can put out the flames, and they block the line itself: with them
  burning the boulder cannot pass even if the belt runs.
- **Fire** is the only one who can melt the ice, and the only one who can light the torch. The torch
  is past the ice, so the order is forced.
- Neither of them can be the weight. That is the whole level.

## The insight

The plate is in plain sight from the start, with the vault behind it, and the obvious thing to try
is to stand on it — then to try it with both of you. Neither does anything, and it is not a matter
of trying harder: a heavy plate reads the heaviest single thing on it. In a level with no Earth in
it, the only heavy thing is the boulder, and the only thing that can move a boulder without Earth is
the belt. Everything else in the level exists to start that belt.

## Likely failures and how players recover

- **Standing on the plate.** Costs nothing but the time to realise. The sign says as much.
- **Lighting the torch before melting the ice.** The boulder sets off and jams against the ice. Melt
  it and the boulder carries on — the belt never stops, so nothing is lost.
- **Dawdling on the belt.** The boulder catches up and shoves you along in front of it. Walk off at
  the end; the belt is a floor, not a trap, and there is no pit under it.
- Nothing in this level can be made unwinnable.

## Shortcuts that were closed

- The flames and the ice are both three tiles tall under rock, so neither can be jumped.
- The vault is three tiles tall under rock, and nothing but the plate opens it.
- Neither player can hold the plate, alone or together, or by standing on each other: a plate takes
  the largest mass overlapping it and ignores riders. Verified in the simulation, not assumed.
- There is no second route to the far end: the hall is one storey and the belt spans it.

## Kinship with the campaign

Box Relay and Last Shift both put a load on a belt and a plate at the end of it. What they never
do is make the plate one the players themselves cannot hold — in those levels somebody stands on
something, and here nobody can.
