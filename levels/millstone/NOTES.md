# Millstone (`millstone`) — Water + Earth + Air

**Introduces:** a belt driven by a turbine, carrying something that cannot walk.

1. **Solution.** Water hops for its gem and puts out the flames that shut the hall, then steps
   back out of the way with Air. Earth takes its own gem and shoulders the boulder along the floor
   until it is sitting on the belt. Air returns to the turbine — the only thing in the level it
   alone can reach — and spins it; the belt runs for seven seconds, which is more than enough to
   carry the boulder the ten tiles to the heavy plate at the far end. The gate opens. Air hops for
   the gem four tiles above the belt, which again only Air can reach, picks up the neutral gem and
   all three walk to their doors.

2. **Roles.** Water opens the hall. Earth is the only one who can move the boulder at all. Air is
   the only one who can start the belt.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The flames run from the floor to the roof and only Water puts them
   out. A boulder is mass three: only Earth pushes it, and only it is heavy enough for the plate.
   A turbine answers to Air and nobody else. `check` confirms all three counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The boulder does not have to be pushed the whole way — it only has to be
   pushed as far as the belt, and the belt is somebody else's job.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** The turbine can be spun as often as you like, so a boulder
   that stops halfway just needs another spin. The boulder cannot be lost: pushed past the belt
   it sits on open floor. There is no liquid or deadly tile anywhere in the level, and nothing
   has to be timed.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The flames and the gate both reach the roof. The turbine
  is four tiles up, past Water's and Earth's jumps, and the belt has no other input. The plate at
  the end is heavy, so no player and no crate will hold it — and the boulder is the only mass in
  the level.
