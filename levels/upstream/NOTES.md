# Upstream (`upstream`) — Fire + Earth

**Introduces:** beating a belt that is faster than you are. Walking against this belt gains nobody
anything — it runs at 3.3 pixels a tick against Fire's 3.2 and Earth's 2.6 — but a hop breaks
contact with it for a moment, and a string of hops crosses the whole tunnel even under a roof one
tile high.

1. **Solution.** Fire hops up for its gem, walks to the ice at the mouth of the tunnel and melts
   it. Then it hops the fourteen tiles of belt, one hop at a time, hops over the boulder at the
   far end and stands clear. Earth follows the same way, taking the neutral gem in the tunnel and
   its own gem beyond it, lands on the far side of the boulder and shoves it onto the heavy plate.
   The gate opens and both walk to their doors.

2. **Roles.** Fire opens the tunnel; nothing else melts ice. Earth is the only one who can move
   the boulder, and the only mass the plate accepts.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The ice is two tiles with rock to the roof above it, so Earth never
   gets into the tunnel without Fire; the plate at the end is heavy, so Fire alone never opens the
   gate. `check` confirms both counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The belt is not a wall, it is a floor that keeps moving — and a player who is
   not standing on it is not being carried by it. The roof is one tile high, so the hop is almost
   nothing, which is exactly the point: it does not have to be a jump, only a moment off the ground.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Walking into the tunnel simply slides you back out of it,
   which costs time and nothing else; there is no liquid or deadly tile anywhere. The boulder
   cannot be lost: pushed past the plate it sits against the tunnel's mouth, where it can be
   pushed back. Both players hop over the boulder rather than past it, so neither can be shut out
   by it.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The tunnel is the only route east: its roof runs from row
  10 to row 18 over every belt tile, so there is nothing to climb over and no room above the belt
  for anything. The ice reaches the roof, and the gate is two tiles with rock above it. The
  boulder sits beyond the belt, so it can never be fed onto it and ridden.
