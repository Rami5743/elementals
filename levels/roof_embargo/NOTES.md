# Roof Embargo

Batch-90: 77/90

**Introduces:** the same retractable slab serves as Earth's upper bridge and the ceiling that prevents a pound. A remote selector must alternate four times because the other workshops use complementary permissions.

## The intended solution

1. Earth crosses the closed slab and records the upper certificate.
2. Water's first workshop opens; its certificate releases Fire's roof console.
3. Fire opens the slab after Earth returns to the mouth. Earth drops into the crawl, collects the gem and pounds the panel.
4. Earth records the lower western certificate. Fire closes the roof; this releases Water's second workshop.
5. Water's second certificate and the closed roof allow Earth to enter the lower eastern chamber.
6. Earth's eastern certificate releases Fire's far workshop.
7. Fire returns to the console and opens the roof, allowing Water to certify the final workshop.
8. Fire closes it one last time so Water can return west across the now-open return gate and release Fire and Earth.

## Why each player is needed

Earth alone can pound the cracked panel and is isolated from the selector. Water's separate workshop certificates authorize the roof console, Earth's lower chamber and the other players' exits. Fire alone can reach the remote roof selector and the far workshop.

## The insight

The ceiling is not simply a wall: it is a necessary bridge before becoming a veto on the pound. Neither permanently closed nor permanently open is a useful policy. The same selector is used later for complementary permissions, and the final open state must be undone to bring the receiver home.

## Likely failures and how players recover

Attempting to pound in the one-tile crawl fails; the separate console can retract the ceiling while Earth waits safely below. The recovery replay explicitly attempts the closed-roof pound, verifies the panel is intact and subsequently completes after the console is operated. Retaining the open roof after the descent blocks the second workshop, but the console is still reachable. The upper certificate is required before the console can be reached; no one can destroy the bridge before that certificate. There are no hazards or timed inputs. The lower pedestal has generous landing room on either side.

## Shortcuts that were closed

A pound under the closed slab and leaving the roof open after descent both fail in scripted attempts. The separated galleries and full-height doors block substitution.

## Kinship with the campaign

Eclipse uses a cracked floor to reach a vault, but its pound is not controlled by the headroom. Sealed Descent at three players uses cracked-floor certificates, not a movable ceiling. Adapted from the four-player Crawlspace, but here the other players directly control the roof and alternate its complementary permissions rather than moving a boulder.
