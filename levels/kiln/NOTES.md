# Kiln (`kiln`) — Fire + Earth + Air

**Introduces:** a gale — a fan strong enough to lift Fire, and Fire with Earth standing on it.

1. **Solution.** Fire plants itself in the flue and Earth climbs onto its head. Air runs to the
   button at the far end of the hall and presses it: the gale blows for eight seconds. Fire and
   Earth go straight up sixteen tiles, taking the neutral gem, and steer out into the loft. Air,
   waiting at the foot of the flue, follows them up once they are clear. In the loft Fire melts
   the ice, Air spins the turbine — which latches the gate open — and Earth walks through and
   ground-pounds the cracked floor. All three drop into the vault, where the doors are.

2. **Roles.** Air starts the gale and spins the turbine; nothing else does either. Fire is the lift
   — a gale at this strength carries Fire and one passenger, and nothing carries Earth otherwise.
   Earth is the only one who can break the vault floor.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The loft is sixteen tiles above the hall with solid rock either side
   of the flue. Earth never rises in a draught of its own accord, Fire cannot pass the turbine's
   gate, and the vault has no other way in. `check` confirms all three counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** A gale does not lift a player, it lifts a stack — and the stack has to be
   built before the button is pressed, because the clock starts the moment it is.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** The window is the whole difficulty: eight seconds covers the
   stack's climb and Air's with room to spare, but not a stack built from scratch afterwards. The
   button can be pressed again as often as you like, and there is no liquid or deadly tile in the
   level, so a missed window costs a walk back down the hall. The cracked floor is one-way, and
   the doors are below it.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The flue's walls are rock from row 8 to row 14. The ice
  and the loft gate both run to the roof, the turbine is five tiles clear of the gate so nobody
  brushes it in passing, and the gate latches so the loft stays open once it has been opened.
