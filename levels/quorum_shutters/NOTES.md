# Quorum Shutters

**Introduces:** a complementary bridge pair requiring two enabling keys; the third operator must abandon its key to certify the route.

## The intended solution
Earth enables the quorum while Fire and Water exchange the upper and lower keys to cross the first shutter. Fire reaches the far contact and Water operates the far relay. Earth now turns off its key, crosses its released booth gate, certifies the first exchange and returns to restore its key. Fire and Water exchange the shutters in reverse to obtain their left contacts. Earth abandons the key again to obtain the second certificate. Only after Earth returns to enable the quorum can Fire and Water repeat the outward handoff. Water temporarily leaves its exit to return Fire to the left. Earth releases its key after Fire is home and exits through the two final permissions.

## Why each player is needed
Fire and Water travel opposite sides of the moving bridges; Earth operates the enabling key and provides two mandatory certificates. All three must change state at least once.

## The insight
A key can be a prerequisite for someone else's movement and an impediment to its owner's movement. The two travelling players must remain safe while the third temporarily withdraws permission.

## Likely failures and how players recover
The first two operators must transfer the live key before releasing it. Earth restores its key after each certification. If a shutter is in the wrong state, return to the middle lever or use the ledges to recover; no timed jump is required.

## Shortcuts that were closed
Direct walking, omitting the handoff, and retaining the lower key during the lower crossing fail.

## Kinship with the campaign
Extends Shutter Exchange with a separately trapped quorum operator, rather than merely adding a parallel destination.
