# Sluicegate (`sluicegate`) — Water + Earth + Air

**Introduces:** two timers that have to overlap — a sluice that stays open five seconds and a
stretch of water that stays frozen ten.

1. **Solution.** Water hops for its gem and stands on the freeze pad: the sluice freezes over.
   Earth takes up position at the closed gate. Air hops into the turbine, which only Air can
   reach, and the gate opens for five seconds — Earth, the slowest of the three, goes first,
   running the gate and the ice and taking the neutral gem in the middle of it, then shoulders the
   boulder on to the heavy plate. Air spins the turbine a second time; Water leaves the pad and
   both of them run the crossing on the ice it left behind, Air hopping for its gem over the
   sluice on the way. With the boulder on the plate the last gate stands open and all three walk
   to their doors.

2. **Roles.** Water makes the crossing possible and is the last to use it. Air is the only one who
   can open the sluice, and has to open it twice. Earth is the only mass the far plate accepts.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The turbine is five tiles up, past Earth's and Water's jumps and
   inside Air's; the gate and the last door are both three tiles with rock to the roof above them;
   the sluice is eight tiles of water that drowns Earth and Air; and the last plate is heavy, so
   only the boulder — which only Earth can push — holds it. `check` confirms all three
   counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** Send the slowest player through the short window first, and spend the second
   window on the two who are fast enough to share it.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Missing the window shuts someone in the sluice, which is
   recoverable as long as the ice holds — and the ice holds ten seconds after Water steps off the
   pad, twice the gate's own timer, so the ordering is forgiving. Stepping into unfrozen water
   drowns Earth or Air and restarts the level. The turbine can be spun again as often as needed,
   and the boulder, pushed too far, sits on open floor.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** Both doors run from the floor to the roof. The turbine is
  eight tiles from the gate, so nobody can brush it while squeezing through, and it is the only
  input either gate has. The boulder stands on the far side of the sluice, where it cannot be used
  as a step to anything.
