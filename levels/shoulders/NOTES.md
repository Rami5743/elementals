# Shoulders (`shoulders`) — all four

**Introduces:** a switch that only Air can work, placed where Air cannot get. The turbine hangs
five tiles above the floor; Air's jump is measured at 3.75 tiles and nothing else in the game
answers to a turbine at all, so the one player who can use it is the one who needs a leg up.

1. **Solution.** Water puts out the flames that shut the hall and takes the two gems on that side.
   Earth plants itself on the mark under the turbine; Air climbs onto Earth's head and, from
   there, jumps into the turbine — its reach from a partner's head is 4.7 tiles, which is enough.
   The gate at the end of the hall latches open. Fire walks through, takes its gem and melts the
   ice beyond; Earth takes its own gem, ground-pounds the cracked floor, and all four drop into
   the vault.

2. **Roles.** Water opens the hall, Earth is the step and the only one who can open the vault, Air
   is the only one the turbine answers to, Fire opens the ice.

3. **Why each is necessary.** Measured in the game's own simulation: Air's jump from the floor
   puts its body at 492–520 pixels, and the turbine's lowest edge is at 384 — out of reach. From a
   partner's head Air reaches 366. The flames, the ice and the cracked floor are each one
   element's business. `check` confirms all four counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The level reverses the usual rule. Air is normally the one who reaches what
   nobody else can; here it is the one who cannot reach, and the heavy, low-jumping Earth is what
   makes the difference.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Nothing in the level is deadly and the gate latches once
   spun, so the climb only has to work once. A missed climb drops Air on open floor beside Earth.
   The only one-way move is the pound at the end, and the doors are under it.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The turbine's only output is the gate, which is three
  tiles with rock to the roof above it; the flames and the ice are the same. The turbine is
  fourteen tiles from the gate, so nobody brushes it in passing, and there is no crate, boulder or
  ledge anywhere in the hall to stand on instead of a partner.

- **Kinship with the campaign.** Turbines appear throughout the campaign (24, 48, 73) and l17 has
  Earth as a ladder, but in every one of them Air is the player who reaches what the others
  cannot. Here that is turned around.
