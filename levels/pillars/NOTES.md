# Pillars (`pillars`) — Fire + Water + Earth

**Introduces:** the plain human ladder. A jump clears two tiles; a jump from a partner's head
clears three, and the shelf is at three.

1. **Solution.** Water hops for its gem and puts out the flames that shut the hall, then steps
   back out of the way. Earth takes its gem and shoulders the boulder along the floor onto the
   heavy plate, which opens the inner gate. Water walks through, hops for the neutral gem and
   plants itself under the shelf; Fire climbs onto its head and jumps again, landing three tiles
   up on the shelf. Up there Fire melts the ice, takes its gem and pulls the lever that opens the
   last gate. Fire walks off the far end of the shelf, drops into the hall and all three go to
   their doors — Water and Earth having walked under the shelf through the tunnel.

2. **Roles.** Water opens the hall and is the step. Earth is the only mass the heavy plate takes.
   Fire is the only one who can melt the ice and so reach the lever.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The shelf is three tiles above the floor, out of reach of any single
   jump, and nothing in the level can be climbed or stacked except a partner. The flames are
   Water's, the heavy plate is Earth's, the ice is Fire's. `check` confirms all three
   counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The shelf and the tunnel under it are the same wall seen twice: two of you go
   under it and one of you goes over, and the one who goes over needs the other two to get there.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Nothing in the level is deadly and nothing can be lost: the
   boulder stops against the plate and can be pushed back if it overshoots, and a missed climb
   drops Fire onto open floor. The only ordering that matters is that Water should stand still
   while Fire is on its head.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The flames, the inner gate and the last gate all run from
  the floor to the roof. The shelf is reachable only from above, and the hall below it has no
  crate or boulder that could be used as a step — the only boulder is pinned on the plate four
  tiles further back, and it is one tile tall, two short of what the shelf needs.
