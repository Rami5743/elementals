# Silence Convoy

Batch-90: 72/90

**Introduces:** two independently moving cargo clocks create a shared absence of vetoes for a two-person convoy; the heavy traveller releases a wind-only inspector.

## The intended solution
1. Fire launches the slow cargo clock.
2. Water observes the cue contact and launches the faster clock later.
3. The four long veto contacts become simultaneously silent.
4. Earth and Air cross the silence gate in the same window.
5. Earth reaches the heavy plate, latching the inspector's gate.
6. Air crosses and activates the turbine. Together with the far cargo contacts this latches both remote workshop doors.
7. Fire and Water record the distant workshop certificates and return.
8. Both cargo clocks return home, releasing the exits. Air exits first, then Earth.

## Why each player is needed
Fire and Water independently launch and inspect the clocks. Earth alone activates the heavy plate; Air alone powers the turbine.

## The insight
The opening is an intersection of empty contact intervals rather than active signals. Both downstream roles must exploit that shared window and then perform physically distinct tasks.

## Likely failures and how players recover
The launch buttons remain accessible. A mistaken simultaneous launch leaves the gate closed and both stones return to their docks. A recovery replay checks the inspector gate remains closed, repeats the launches and finishes. A replay delaying the second launch 90 ticks finishes too. A traveller on either side can wait for the next launch. No hazards.

## Shortcuts that were closed
Simultaneous launches fail. Air without Earth's heavy certificate fails. All four idle-at-exit attempts fail.

## Kinship with the campaign
Clockwork overlaps active timed inputs; here physical gaps overlap. Falling Dispatch uses a falling pulse, Phase Dispatch uses opposing strokes. Neither uses two independent cargo clocks to create silence for a convoy. Silent Interval at three players has a single traveller with a lever; here two travellers have complementary heavy and wind operations.
