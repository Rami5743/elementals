# Sealed Descent

Batch-90: 58/90

**Introduces:** A cracked-floor descent requires two independent authorizations. The inspector's one reversible seal enables the first hatch approach but blocks the second, and cannot remain in place after the first floor has been spent.

## Intended solution
1. Water grants the first east permission.
2. Earth records its upper certificate and returns west, releasing the inspector's seal workshop and Water's second station.
3. Water revokes the first permission and grants the west permission. Fire sets the seal; both inputs are needed for the first hatch approach.
4. Earth breaks the first hatch and records the middle left certificate.
5. Water withdraws the west permission. Fire must withdraw the seal to reach the next station, and then certify the joint authorization for Water's third control.
6. Water grants the middle east route. Earth certifies the second hatch, which is now accessible because the seal is off, and breaks it.
7. Earth records the bottom certificate and Fire returns west to record the independent final inspector certificate.
8. Water withdraws the third permission and returns west, where both certificates are needed to reach the final control.
9. Water grants Earth's bottom east route; Earth crosses and releases the final exits. All three collect their gems and finish.

## Why each player is needed
Earth alone drills the floors and records the geological certificates. Water controls the reversible movement permissions. Fire controls the separate seal: its first setting is necessary for the first descent and the opposite setting is necessary for the second, and Fire supplies two independent authorizations to Water.

## Insight
The inspector's single seal cannot authorize both hatches. The first floor must be spent while the seal is active, then the seal must be withdrawn before the next floor can be spent; the other operator's route permissions alone are insufficient.

## Recovery
Every permission can be restored from its control. Removing the seal too soon simply closes the first approach; restoring it lets Earth proceed. This mistake was simulated. The first hatch is accessible only after the upper certificate, and the second is behind its own certificate gate. There is no lethal terrain, and a missed ground pound can be retried on a three-tile panel.

## Shortcuts
Retaining the seal, retaining Water's first permission, and leaving the second floor intact all fail in simulation. The galleries are isolated and gates reach their ceilings.

## Kinship
Drill (four players) uses stacked floor hatches. Descent Ledger (two players) uses a dispatcher who alternates movement permissions. In this three-player version the third player is a separate inspector whose single seal is physically incompatible between the two hatch approaches. Pound-chain is new at three players.
