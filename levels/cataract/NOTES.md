# Cataract (`cataract`) — all four

**Introduces:** a ford that one player makes passable for the other three, with three more locks
behind it, one for each of them.

1. **Solution.** Water stands on the pad at the near bank and the ford freezes over. Fire crosses
   the ice, taking the neutral gem, and melts the wall of ice beyond it; Air follows, hops into
   the turbine that nobody else can reach, which latches the gate open, and takes its gem four
   tiles up. Earth walks through, takes its gem and ground-pounds the cracked floor at the far
   end. All four drop into the vault, Water coming last across its own ice and picking up its gem
   on the way.

2. **Roles.** Water makes the crossing, Fire opens the wall, Air opens the gate, Earth opens the
   floor. Nobody can do anybody else's job.

3. **Why each is necessary.** Seven tiles of water drown everyone but Water; the wall of ice and
   the gate both run from the floor to the roof and answer only to Fire and Air respectively; and
   the vault under the far end has no entrance but the cracked floor. `check` confirms all four
   counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** Water goes last. The pad is on the near bank, so the player who makes the
   crossing possible is the one who cannot use it until everybody else has — and the ice holds
   fifteen seconds after Water steps off, which is the margin the level gives you.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Stepping onto an unfrozen ford drowns Fire, Earth or Air and
   restarts; the ford is plainly drawn and the pad is three tiles from its edge. Nothing else in
   the level can be lost: the turbine latches its gate, the ice is gone for good, and the cracked
   floor is the last thing anyone does.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The ford is seven tiles wide against a three-tile jump,
  and there is nothing to push into it. The turbine sits five tiles clear of the gate it opens,
  so nobody brushes it while squeezing through, and it is four tiles up, past every jump but
  Air's. The vault's roof is solid everywhere except the three cracked tiles.
