# Takeoff Weight

**Groups:** Fire + Water

1. **Solution:** Fire takes the gem and melts the ice in front of Water's door. Water jumps the lava and puts out the flames in front of Fire's door. Both go home.
2. **Roles:** Fire melts the ice. Water puts out the flames.
3. **Why each is necessary:** The ice only melts for Fire and the flames only go out for Water. Both barriers sit under a roof, so nobody can jump them.
4. **Insight:** Your partner's door is your job.
5. **Failure/recovery:** Water dies in the lava and the level restarts. Nothing can be lost or locked.
