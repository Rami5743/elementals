# Half-Weight Keeper

Batch-90: 70/90

**Introduces:** Air's fractional rope weight is exactly the missing ballast for a stable pan; Fire's full unit supplies the movement and is needed away from the pan to ignite the remote torch. Two separate uses of this division of mechanical and electrical roles are separated by a necessary full unload.

## The intended solution
1. Air boards the ballasted pan; its half-unit balances the opposing stone divided by the 2:1 snatch block.
2. Fire joins, lowering their pan and lifting the stone to the high contact.
3. Fire dismounts and lights the remote torch while Air stays on the pan, maintaining the high permission. The torch also preserves the route for Fire's return.
4. Air unloads, letting the stone descend to the low contact. Water can now obtain the first certificate.
5. Water's certificate releases Earth; Earth authorizes the second high workshop.
6. Air rides the recall lift to the upper pan, then Fire recalls and joins it; the pan lowers again.
7. Air maintains the second high contact while Fire reaches the far certificate, authorizing the next low workshop.
8. Air unloads again. The low contact permits Water's second certificate, which releases Air's upper exit, Fire and Earth.
9. Earth sets the last release, permitting Water's exit. Air takes the recall lift back to the upper exit.

## Why each player is needed
The initial torch is Fire-only, so Fire cannot be the permanent counterweight. Air holds the balanced state while Fire travels beyond the distance reachable before the gate closes. Water and Earth each occupy isolated workshops and provide successive low/high permissions. All four idle-at-exit tests fail.

## The insight
Air is not simply a small version of Fire: on a rope it weighs one half, and the ballasted pan exploits that exact difference. Maintaining a permission and changing it are different jobs. The pan must be entirely empty between uses; leaving the keeper aboard is wrong.

## Likely failures and how players recover
If Air unloads after Fire enters the first chamber, the door shuts, but Fire can light the torch from inside to reopen it; a recovery replay deliberately waited for the closure and then completed. If Fire cannot enter yet, both can reuse the independent recall lift; its dwell is 300 ticks. The pan and recall shaft have safe bottom floors and no dangerous terrain. All subsequent certificates are reusable or latch.

## Shortcuts that were closed
Fire's direct rush to the torch without a balancing rider physically stops at the first door; keeping that rider aboard indefinitely also fails.

## Kinship with the campaign
Ballast uses weight to lift Earth; Makeweight compares the arithmetic of a beam and a heavy contact. Here a half-unit rider holds a ballasted rope stable while the indispensable Fire-only ignition is performed remotely, and an intervening low state requires the same pair to board again.
