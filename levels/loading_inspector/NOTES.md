# Loading Inspector

**Introduces:** the loading stop has its own operator, trapped in the enabling booth while the stop is engaged.

## The intended solution
Air engages the loading stop and Water sends the stone right to load Fire's shuttle. Fire makes the first stripped delivery, releasing the inspector's first gate. Air opens the stop to leave the booth and certifies the delivery. Earth opens the stripper and Water returns the stone left. Air re-engages the loading stop; Water reloads the home shuttle, Fire delivers intact, and the upper contact and Fire's relay release the next certificates. Air opens the stop again and certifies while Earth closes the stripper. Fire returns the shuttle and the closed stripper drops the stone to the lower belt. Water returns the stone left to certify the second stripped journey. Air obtains its final certificate and returns to engage the stop; Earth opens the stripper and Water reloads the shuttle. Fire makes the last intact delivery and all four exit.

## Why each player is needed
Fire moves the shuttle; Water reverses the belt; Earth operates the stripper; Air controls the loading stop and performs mandatory inspections. Their separate galleries prevent substitution.

## The insight
Loading is a deliberate interaction between the belt and a stop. Engaging the stop traps its operator, so inspections must occur with the stop open. Closing the stripper on the return changes the destination of the cargo without obstructing the shuttle.

## Likely failures and how players recover
Return the shuttle home and reverse the belt left before reloading if the cargo is misplaced. If the stripper was left open, close it and repeat the shuttle journey. Restore or release the loading stop according to the phase. No timed movement is required.

## Shortcuts that were closed
Direct walking, leaving the stripper open on return, sending the shuttle empty, and omitting the loading stop all fail the scripted checks.

## Kinship with the campaign
Develops Stripping Foreman by separating the loading stop from the belt and requiring an inspector to leave and return to the enabling booth.
