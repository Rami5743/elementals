# Draught Dispatcher

Batch-90: 50/90

**Introduces:** Overlapping weak and strong fans make three useful configurations. Earth has their controls, and only Air can initially travel up the weak current.

## Intended solution
1. Earth issues the initial permission; Air rises alone and issues its upper permission.
2. Fire reaches the lower switch, jointly releasing Earth's strong-current selector.
3. Earth enables the strong fan. Fire ascends and melts the ice guarding the first upper certificate.
4. Earth disables the stronger fan. Fire descends through the weak current and gives the lower certificate.
5. Earth receives both certificates, visits the distant authorization switch and unlocks Air's second upper workshop.
6. Air gives the second authorization; Earth returns to the selector and restores the stronger current.
7. Fire collects the second upper certificate and gem. Earth cuts the strong current again.
8. Fire descends and earns the final lower certificate. Earth can now shut the weak current off as well, allowing Air down. All exit.

## Why each player is needed
Air alone reaches the initial upper controls on the weak current. Fire melts the single ice barrier and completes the high/low certificates. Earth is physically isolated with both fan controls and the reciprocal certificate.

## The insight
A reduced current and a stopped current are different states. The first sends Fire down while still supporting Air; the second finally sends Air down as well. The separate dispatcher must choose which is useful.

## Likely failures and recovery
Leaving the strong current on strands Fire upstairs; reduce it at the selector. Leaving the weak current on strands Air; the final Earth control stops it. Both modes are reversible; the interrupted first ascent followed by restoration was simulated. Safe wide banks and a full-height shaft allow retries with no deadline.

## Shortcuts
The direct route, retaining the strong current and retaining the weak current fail. Earth cannot cross solid separation; the only elemental barrier is the ice, and workshop gates span their galleries.

## Kinship
Selective Draught uses two cooperating travelers at two players. Here the third operator holds both currents and receives a joint high/low authorization before the second ascent. Fan-band is new at three players.
