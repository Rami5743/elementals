# Gangway (`gangway`) — Fire + Earth + Air

## The intended solution

Three tiles of the hall floor are missing, and over the sludge below there is a
lift platform parked flush with the floor. It is the only way across, and it is
also the only way up to the loft. It cannot be both at once.

1. **Fire** walks out onto the platform while it is still a floor.
2. **Air** steps on the turbine. The platform becomes a lift and carries Fire up
   the shaft to the loft — and the road across the hall is now a hole.
3. **Fire** steps off into the loft, melts the ice, takes its gem and throws the
   lever at the far end. The vault door in the hall latches open.
4. **Air** leaves the turbine and goes to its own door. The platform comes back
   down and the hall is a hall again.
5. **Earth** crosses the gangway, walks through the vault door and pounds the
   cracked floor, dropping into the vault.
6. **Fire** comes back down the shaft onto the platform, crosses, and drops in
   after Earth.

## Why each player is needed

- **Air** is the only one who can spin the turbine, so Air alone decides whether
  the platform is a lift or a floor. Nothing in the level happens without that
  decision being made twice.
- **Fire** is the only one who can melt the ice in front of the lever, and the
  lever is the only thing that opens the vault.
- **Earth** is the only one who can break the cracked floor, which is the only
  way into the vault.

## The insight

Every other lift in the game is a thing you wait for. This one is the ground you
are standing on. Sending it up is not free — it takes the road away, so whoever
is going to need that road has to be on the right side of it first, and whoever
is going up has to already be aboard.

## Likely failures and how players recover

- **Air spinning the turbine with nobody aboard.** The platform goes up empty and
  comes straight back down when Air steps off. Nothing is lost.
- **Earth walking into the gap while the lift is up.** It falls in the sludge.
  Wait for the platform; the hall is flat and the gap is obvious.
- **Fire coming down the shaft while the lift is up.** It lands on the platform
  and rides back down with it — this is fine, just slower.
- **Throwing the lever and then forgetting Fire.** The vault latches open, so
  Fire can come down at any time afterwards.

## Shortcuts that were closed

- The hall is two tiles high everywhere, so the three-tile gap cannot be jumped
  even with a run-up.
- The shaft above the gap is three tiles wide and seven deep, with rock on both
  sides; the only way into the loft is on the platform.
- The loft is a dead end: its floor stops at the shaft, so Fire cannot walk along
  it to the far side.
- The vault door is latched by the lever, so the loft only has to be visited
  once.

## Kinship with the campaign

The campaign's lift levels are about catching a lift that will not wait (Catch
the Lift, Paternoster, Brake). Here the lift never leaves without you — the cost
of sending it is that the floor goes with it.
