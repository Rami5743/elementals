## Why each player is needed

Each gallery supplies remote occupancy signals. The full-height rock separation means a different element cannot stand in for the missing operator. All four idle-at-exit tests fail.

## The insight

A held plate is a volatile key. A jointly activated latch remembers a prior coincidence, so the players can spend that position to acquire another permission. Reversing the route is necessary.

## Likely failures and how players recover

The direct and forward-only routes both fail. Revisit the room with the required plate, or step off a plate to release an inverted gate. The abstract room-and-plate model found 18785 reachable states, all 18785 with a path to completion. This is not an exhaustive continuous-physics proof. No timed gates, lethal gaps or precision jumps are needed.

## Shortcuts that were closed

The direct and forward-only scripts fail. Full-height doors and separate galleries prevent head boosts, partner substitution and body-doorstops.

## Kinship with the campaign

Interlock uses remote keys; this version makes actual occupancy the temporary key and latches a permanent record of a meeting. It extends Footprint Ledger and Attendance to four histories.
