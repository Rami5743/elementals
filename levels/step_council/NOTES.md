# Step Council

Batch-90: 52/90

**Introduces:** Two coupled travelling steps have separate inspectors and operators. The inspector cannot change the geometry; the selector operator cannot authorize the next bank workshops.

## Intended solution
1. Water enables the initial workshops. Fire and Earth use their crates to reach opposite banks.
2. The paired certificates release Air's first inspection; Air grants Water access to the selector.
3. Water reverses both crates. Both climbers drop, follow the steps and collect the opposite-bank certificates.
4. Air inspects the pair and authorizes the next initial-side workshops.
5. Water restores the orientation; the climbers earn the next paired certificates. Air authorizes the following opposite banks.
6. Water reverses again; the climbers reach the second opposite workshops. Air authorizes the third initial workshops.
7. Water restores the initial geometry for the last initial-bank visit. Air inspects the pair and opens the final opposite workshops.
8. Water reverses the steps one last time. Fire and Earth collect their gems and final joint certificate.
9. Water obtains its gem and releases Air's final door; all exit.

## Why each player is needed
Fire and Earth occupy separate trenches and provide paired certificates. Water alone moves the coupled steps; Air alone opens the next workshops after the previous paired tasks. Solid rock separates all four roles.

## The insight
A crate provides missing height rather than an electrical signal. The inspector's permissions and the selector's geometry are distinct resources. Neither control alone suffices; the next paired visit needs both.

## Likely failures and recovery
If Water selects the wrong orientation, reverse it. The crates are retained in their trenches. Missed climbs can be retried safely; no deadline applies. An interrupted reversal and restoration were simulated successfully.

## Shortcuts
Retaining the wrong orientation and trying to climb the bare trench fail. Full-height doors and broad gaps block direct bypasses.

## Kinship
Opposite Steps uses one operator at three players. Here authorization and geometry are split between two isolated roles; the joint inspection chain unlocks a sixth visit and the final opposite banks. Crate-step is new at four players.
