# The Mill (`the_mill`) — all four

**Introduces:** a belt that runs the wrong way on purpose — the heavy plate is behind you, and the
belt is the only thing that can carry a boulder back to it.

1. **Solution.** Water puts out the flames that shut the hall and takes its gem. All four walk
   east across the belt while it is still, collecting the gems along it. Earth works round to the
   far side of the boulder and shoves it onto the belt; Air, standing clear, hops into the turbine
   and the belt runs west, carrying the boulder ten tiles back to the heavy plate. The gate opens,
   Fire melts the ice beyond it, Earth ground-pounds the cracked floor and all four drop into the
   vault.

2. **Roles.** Water opens the hall, Earth loads the belt and opens the vault, Air drives the belt,
   Fire opens the ice.

3. **Why each is necessary.** A boulder is mass three: only Earth moves it and only it satisfies
   the heavy plate. A turbine answers only to Air, the flames only to Water and the ice only to
   Fire. `check` confirms all four counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** Pushing the boulder all the way back would be eleven tiles of work against the
   belt; putting it on the belt is one tile of work and then somebody else's switch.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Cross the belt before it is running — against it a walk gains
   barely a pixel a tick, though a hop still beats it. The belt stops after ten seconds, so
   nothing is permanent except the boulder on its plate. The boulder cannot be lost: it ends in a
   dead end at the west wall. There is no liquid or deadly tile in the level.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The flames, the gate and the ice all reach the roof. The
  turbine is five tiles clear of the gate it does not open and is the belt's only input. The
  boulder's road home runs west, behind everyone, so it never stands in the corridor.
