# Glasshouse (`glasshouse`) — Fire + Water + Earth

**Introduces:** a shuttle lift whose switch is at a dead end behind a barrier, so the crate that
holds it has to be fetched before anyone can ride.

1. **Solution.** Water walks west and puts out the flames that shut the dead end, takes its gem
   and shoves the crate the rest of the way onto the plate: the shuttle starts running. Fire melts
   the ice in front of the shuttle's berth and takes its own gem. All three wait for the shuttle at
   the bottom, ride ten tiles up past the neutral gem and step off into the loft. Earth walks the
   loft, takes its gem, hops over the boulder at the far end and rolls it back onto the heavy
   plate; the gate opens and all three go to their doors.

2. **Roles.** Water opens the dead end and sets the shuttle running. Fire opens the way to the
   shuttle. Earth is the only mass the loft's plate accepts.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The flames and the ice both run from the floor to the roof, and each
   is one element's business. The loft is ten tiles up, so the shuttle is the only way there, and
   the shuttle only runs while the plate at the dead end is held — by the crate, because every
   player has to ride. The loft's plate is heavy, which only the boulder satisfies. `check`
   confirms all three counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** Each of the three opens a door for the next: the flames for the crate, the ice
   for the shuttle, the boulder for the doors — and none of them can do the next one's job.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** The shuttle loops, so a missed boarding costs one pass. The
   crate cannot be lost: it is pushed into the dead end against the west wall. The boulder is
   against the loft's east wall, where Earth has to hop over it to get behind it, and pushed too
   far it only sits on open floor. There is no liquid or deadly tile anywhere.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The loft is ten tiles above the hall, past a jump, a
  head-stand and a two-player boost, and the shaft is capped by a one-way hatch. Both hall
  barriers reach the roof, and the loft gate is four tiles with the roof above it.
