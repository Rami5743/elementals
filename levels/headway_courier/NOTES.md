# Headway Courier

Batch-90: 84/90

**Introduces:** boulder-carry as a courier credential, rather than as a final exit key. The swimmer deposits the stone using a low ceiling, then the pusher gains access to a clock above the river. Two distinct timed deliveries are required, with a round-trip authorization between them.

## Solution
Earth climbs onto the ledge. Water stands at its lip to catch the tipped stone, carries it over the water and walks underneath the low roof to strip it onto the heavy plate. This latches open the lift access. Earth rides the lift and presses the clock while Water waits at the first shutter. Water crosses and operates the first certificate. Earth crosses the upper gallery to authorize the second shutter, then returns to the clock. Water crosses the second shutter on the renewed pulse and stands on the receipt plate, releasing Earth's far exit. Both collect their gems on the way.

## Necessity, shortcuts and recovery
Only Earth pushes the stone from the ledge and only Water carries it across the river. Swimming without transporting the stone, omitting the pulse, and omitting the second pulse are separately tested shortcuts. The lift loops; a missed boarding can be retried. Either missed shutter window is recoverable by another pulse from the same clock. The low roof deposits the stone before the clock stage so the timed stage does not risk losing the stone. Both characters may wait at safe points. The pulse lasts 250 ticks; a 90-tick late departure was also simulated.

## Novelty
The two-player catalogue has no boulder-carry core. The four-player Headroom supplied the physical handoff technique, but here the deposition unlocks a separate two-pulse delivery protocol, and neither element can finish merely by depositing the stone. Unlike Cargo Turnstile, the timed gates carry a player, not the stone, and the clock operator must make an upper-gallery authorization round trip.
