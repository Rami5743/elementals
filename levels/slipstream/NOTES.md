# Slipstream (`slipstream`) — Fire + Earth

**Introduces:** a switch hanging in the air over a chasm. Plates read whatever overlaps them, and
this one overlaps nothing that can stand on it — the only way to press it is to be in flight.

## The intended solution

The two gates — Earth's, at the far left, and the vault at the far right — each want two keys held
at the same moment. One key is the heavy plate on the near bank. The other is the plate hanging
over the chasm.

1. **Fire** hops past the boulder, melts the ice between the boulder and the heavy plate, and then
   gets out of the boulder's road.
2. **Earth** comes in behind the boulder and shoves it left onto the heavy plate. That key is now
   held, and stays held.
3. **Fire** runs at the chasm and leaps. On the way over, its body passes through the plate hanging
   above the gap — the second key, pressed for about a fifth of a second. Both keys are on at once,
   so both gates latch open.
4. Fire lands on the far bank, collects its gem and walks into the vault. Earth hops back over the
   boulder and through the gate the leap opened for it.

## Why each player is needed

- **Earth** is the only one who can move the boulder, and the plate is heavy, so no player can hold
  that key — including Earth itself, which has to end up standing in its own door.
- **Fire** is the only one who can melt the ice, and the only one who can press the airborne plate,
  because the leap is beyond Earth's jump. With Fire idle, Earth's gate never opens either: the leap
  is what unlocks the near side as well as the far one.

## The insight

The lock wants two keys at once and there is only one pair of hands free, so one of the keys has to
be something that holds itself down — and the other has to be pressed by a body that cannot stop
moving. Leaping first is the natural order and it is the wrong one: the airborne press lasts a
moment, so if the boulder is not already on its plate the leap does nothing and Fire is on the wrong
side of a chasm it cannot jump back over.

## Likely failures and how players recover

- **Leaping before the boulder is placed.** Nothing latches and Fire is stranded on the far bank.
  The checkpoint is on the near bank, a step from the edge; respawning there costs the walk back.
- **Jumping too early.** Short of the far bank is the sludge. Same checkpoint, same cost.
- **Fire standing where the boulder has to go.** The boulder stops against it. Step aside and Earth
  carries on pushing; nothing is lost.
- Both gates latch, so neither key has to be held once the leap has happened.

## Shortcuts that were closed

- The heavy plate cannot be held by Fire, by Earth, or by one standing on the other: a plate takes
  the largest single mass overlapping it and ignores riders.
- The airborne plate sits a tile above the highest point of a standing jump from either bank, so it
  cannot be reached by jumping on the spot — only by an arc that is already crossing the gap.
- Earth cannot make the leap: its jump is a third of a tile shorter than Fire's and the far bank is
  level with the near one.
- Both gates are three tiles tall under rock.

## Kinship with the campaign

Leap of Faith and Thermals both ask for a committed jump, and Four Corners wants several plates
held at once. None of them puts a switch where nothing can stand, so that the only thing that will
ever press it is a player who is already in the air and cannot stop.
