# Shoulder Signalman

Batch-90: 51/90

**Introduces:** Two timed turbine dispatches require a third operator's permission; that same permission blocks their next control until the two travelers are safely through.

## Intended solution
1. Water authorizes the first intermediate passage.
2. Earth stands below the upper turbine; Air mounts Earth's head and jumps to the turbine.
3. Earth runs through both the authorized passage and the timed gate, setting the far lever to retain the timed opening.
4. Air collects its west gem and Earth returns for the near certificate.
5. Earth and Air descend through the opened floor shutter. Only now does Water withdraw the first permission and advance to the second.
6. Water enables the lower passage. Air mounts Earth at the second turbine, jumps and produces the second timed pulse.
7. Earth races west and sets the far holding lever; then returns east for its gem and final certificate.
8. Both travelers cross to their exits. Water withdraws the second permission and uses its newly freed route to its own gem and exit.

## Why each player is needed
Air alone spins turbines, Earth supplies the extra height and crosses each timed opening, and Water alone controls the two intermediate passage permissions from an isolated room.

## The insight
Each permission is needed during the transient gate crossing and during the return journey, but prevents the signalman from advancing. It must be removed only after both travelers have finished using that station.

## Motor challenge and recovery
Two 480-tick turbine pulses. Both 90-tick delayed departures were tested, as were deliberate expiry and re-triggering of both pulses. The far lever secures a return path. All floors are safe and permission controls can be restored if withdrawn prematurely.

## Shortcuts
The unassisted jump, omitting the first permission and retaining either station permission fail in simulation. Full-height doors and solid separation preclude bypassing the operator.

## Kinship
Shoulder Dispatch (two players) turns a high turbine pulse into a holding route. The third operator here must authorize each dispatch and subsequently withdraw authorization to advance. Turbine-out-of-reach is new at three players.
