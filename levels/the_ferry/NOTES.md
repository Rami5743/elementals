# The Ferry (`the_ferry`) — Fire + Water

**Introduces:** riding on a partner's head across that partner's own liquid, and using the
passenger's ability while aboard.

1. **Solution.** Fire hops up for its gem on the near bank. Water walks to the edge of the pool
   and stands still; Fire jumps onto Water's head. Water wades out into the pool (the neutral gem
   over the water is at passenger height, so Fire takes it on the way) and stops in front of the
   ice gate that fills the only opening in the middle wall. Fire, still aboard, leans right until
   it touches the ice and melts it. Water wades on to the far bank and stands still; Fire walks
   off its head onto solid ground and parks there. Water hops up for its own gem, hops over Fire
   and walks to the flames in front of the two doors, puts them out, and goes to its door. Fire
   follows and goes to its own.

2. **Roles.** Water is the boat and the fire extinguisher. Fire is the passenger and the key: it
   is the only one who can open the gate standing in the middle of the pool, and it can only get
   there as cargo.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The pool is 10 tiles wide, far past Fire's 3-tile liquid crossing,
   and the only other route is through the full-height wall at column 20 — so Fire cannot reach
   the far bank, the gate or either door without Water underneath it. The ice gate and the flames
   both run from the floor to the roof, so Water cannot pass the gate without Fire melting it and
   neither of them can reach the doors until Water puts the flames out. `check` confirms both
   counterfactuals: with Fire parked in its door Water never gets through the gate, and with Water
   parked in its door Fire never leaves the near bank.

4. **The insight.** A passenger on Water's head rides above the surface, so the pool is safe for
   Fire as long as it never touches down — and that height is exactly what puts the ice gate,
   which sits in the water with no floor to stand on, within Fire's reach. The ride is not a
   taxi; it is the only way to use Fire's ability over water.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Mistiming the hop onto Water's head drops Fire on the bank,
   harmless — try again. Walking off Water's head over the pool, or wading off alone, drowns Fire
   and restarts the level; the pool is plainly drawn and the sign warns about it. Nothing in the
   level can be lost or spent: the ice and the flames are gone for good once removed, Water can
   always wade back for Fire, and there is no state a pair can get stuck in.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** Both barriers are 2 tiles of ice/flame with solid rock
  from row 17 up to the roof, so neither can be jumped, nor climbed from a partner's head
  (`check` agrees). There is no box, boulder or ledge anywhere, so nothing can be used as a step
  or thrown over a wall, and no plate or gate that a body could hold open. Fire cannot cross the
  pool by jumping (10 tiles, against a 3-tile limit) and cannot walk under the wall, because the
  pool floor is solid rock and the wall's only opening is the gate itself.
