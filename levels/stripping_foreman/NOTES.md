# Stripping Foreman

**Introduces:** separating the stripper from the loading controls so three operators must certify the same cargo circuit.

## The intended solution
Water loads the stone while the stripper is closed. Fire sends the shuttle outward; the stripper drops the stone onto the lower belt. Earth gains access to the stripper and opens it, and Water reverses the belt to return the stone. Reload the home shuttle, deliver the stone intact and collect the upper contacts. Earth certifies the upper delivery, then closes the stripper while Fire returns the shuttle. The stone falls back onto the lower belt; Fire and Water collect the resulting permissions. Return the stone left to permit Earth to certify the second stripping, then reopen the stripper and reload the shuttle for its final intact delivery.

## Why each player is needed
Fire controls shuttle movement, Water controls the loading belt and two relay circuits, and Earth controls the stripper and provides mandatory certificates.

## The insight
The overhead shuttle can pass through an obstruction that its cargo cannot. The same stone is required at different heights and must be deliberately unloaded before it can be loaded again.

## Likely failures and how players recover
Leaving the stripper open on return prevents the lower-right certificate; close it and repeat the shuttle journey. If the shuttle is empty, bring it home and use the left belt direction before reloading. All controls can be reversed; no timed movement is required.

## Shortcuts that were closed
Direct walking, leaving the stripper open on the return, and sending the shuttle empty all fail the scripted checks.

## Kinship with the campaign
Develops Strip and Reload by separating the stripper operator from the loader and requiring certifications for both routes.
