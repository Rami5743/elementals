# Lantern Hall (`lantern_hall`) — all four

**Introduces:** Fire as the ferry. Lava is Fire's own liquid, and whoever stands on Fire's head
rides over the surface of it.

1. **Solution.** Water puts out the flames at the near end and takes its gem, then the neutral gem
   in the middle of the dark hall. Fire stands at the lava's edge; Water climbs onto its head and
   Fire wades across, taking its own gem in the lava, and sets Water down on the far bank. Fire
   comes back for Earth and ferries it over the same way. Air, who needs no ferry, clears the four
   tiles of lava with a running jump, takes its gem and hops into the turbine, which latches the
   gate open. Earth takes its gem, ground-pounds the cracked floor and all four drop into the
   vault.

2. **Roles.** Fire is the only one the lava is safe for and so the only ferry. Air is the only one
   who can jump it and the only one who can spin a turbine. Water opens the hall; Earth opens the
   vault.

3. **Why each is necessary.** Four tiles of lava are past Water's three-tile crossing and Earth's
   two, and kill both instantly. The flames, the turbine's gate and the vault floor are each one
   element's business. `check` confirms all four counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The level is dark, so the lava reads mostly as the glow of whoever is standing
   in it — and the one standing in it is the one carrying everybody else.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Stepping into the lava, or off Fire's head in the middle of
   it, kills and restarts. Fire can cross as often as it likes, so a dropped passenger just means
   another trip. Nothing in the level can be spent, and the gate latches once Air has spun the
   turbine.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The lava runs the full height of the floor between the two
  banks with nothing to push into it. The flames and the gate reach the roof, the turbine sits
  five tiles clear of the gate so nobody brushes it in passing, and the far bank's floor is solid
  everywhere but the three cracked tiles.
