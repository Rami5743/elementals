# Beacon Duet

Batch-90: 55/90

**Introduces:** Permanent torch bridges and temporary reciprocal permissions have different lifetimes; the first bridge's far-bank authorization must be withdrawn after Fire has used it, before the party can reach the second pair of galleries.

## Intended solution
1. Water grants Fire access to the first beacon.
2. Fire lights it, constructing Water's bridge.
3. Water crosses east and activates the authorization that unlocks Fire's west workshop but closes its own west return.
4. Fire crosses back west and opens both floor hatches, then descends.
5. Water withdraws the temporary authorization and returns west to descend.
6. Water unlocks the second beacon workshop; Fire crosses east to light it.
7. Water crosses the new bridge and issues the west-workshop certificate.
8. Fire returns west, gets its gem and issues both final permissions.
9. Fire returns east and Water returns west to their exits.

## Why each player is needed
Fire alone can light both beacons and use the Fire hatches and workshops; Water alone can authorize their access and issue the opposite-bank certificate. Solid galleries separate the roles.

## Insight
Permanent construction is not enough to advance; the first permission that permits Fire to use the hatch also blocks Water's hatch, and only after Fire has descended can Water safely revoke it.

## Recovery
All levers can be revisited; if the first authorization is withdrawn prematurely, Water can restore it from the far bank. The floor hatches remain open after the certificate. The beacons never extinguish, and each gap is clearly visible; the party must wait on the bank until the bridge is closed. An unnecessary Fire detour before the first authorization was simulated.

## Shortcuts
Crossing before construction, retaining the first permission, and omitting the second beacon fail in simulation. Water cannot jump the twelve-tile gaps, and the gallery gates reach the ceiling.

## Kinship
Beacon Chain at four players constructs two permanent bridges; here both bridge passages belong to one traveler, and a reversible authorization must be withdrawn before the second pair of galleries can be reached. Torch-bridge is new at two players.
