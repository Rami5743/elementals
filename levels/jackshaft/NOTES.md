# Jackshaft (`jackshaft`) — Fire + Water + Earth

**Introduces:** a lift used as a jack. It is not transport: its job is to carry a boulder up the
shaft and hold it against a plate hanging in the ceiling, and to stay there for the rest of the
level.

## The intended solution

The vault at the far end of the hall is held open by a heavy plate in the roof of the shaft, and
nothing in the hall is tall enough to reach it. The lift is.

1. **Water** puts out the flames, then plants itself at the lever. It works the lever three times
   and never does anything else.
2. **Fire** melts the ice behind the flames — that is the way to the vault, not to the shaft — and
   then walks into the lift's berth.
3. Water throws the lever: the lift carries Fire to the top of the shaft. Fire steps off onto the
   loft, which is the only place its own door is.
4. Water throws the lever back. The lift returns, empty.
5. **Earth** shoves the boulder into the berth and steps back off it.
6. Water throws the lever a third time. The lift rises with the boulder, which presses the heavy
   plate in the ceiling and stays pressed. The vault opens, and Water and Earth walk through it.

## Why each player is needed

- **Earth** is the only one who can move the boulder, and the plate is heavy, so nothing lighter
  will hold it.
- **Fire** melts the ice; and Fire is the one for whom the ride matters, because its door is up on
  the loft.
- **Water** puts out the flames, and somebody has to be at the lever at three different moments.

## The insight

Riding the lift up and standing on the plate yourself works — and leaves you standing in the ceiling
for the rest of the level instead of in your door. The plate is heavy, so the only player who could
do it is Earth, which is exactly the player who must be free. The lift has to take something up that
does not mind staying, which means the boulder, which means the lift has to come back down once it
has delivered Fire. The order is the whole puzzle: the moment the boulder is up, the lift is spent.

## Likely failures and how players recover

- **Sending the boulder up before Fire has ridden.** The vault opens, but Fire is stranded in the
  hall. Throw the lever again: the lift comes down with the boulder still on it, the vault shuts,
  Fire rides up, and the boulder goes back. Nothing is lost but the trip.
- **Earth standing in the berth when the lever is thrown.** Earth rides up and holds the plate with
  its own weight — the vault opens, but Earth is in the ceiling. Come back down and use the boulder.
- **Nobody at the lever.** Nothing happens at all; the lever is the only thing that moves the lift.

## Shortcuts that were closed

- The shaft is sealed from the hall by rock from row 10 to row 12: from the hall floor the loft is
  six tiles up, and no jump, and no jump from a partner's head, comes near it.
- The ceiling plate is heavy, so neither Fire nor Water can press it, and a stack does not help —
  a plate takes the largest single mass overlapping it.
- The plate sits over the two shaft tiles only, so standing on the loft beside the shaft does not
  press it.
- The flames and the ice are both three tiles tall under rock, and they guard the vault, not the
  shaft, so they cannot be skipped by going up.

## Kinship with the campaign

Dumbwaiter and Brake both put a boulder on a lift, and both are about getting the boulder somewhere.
Here the boulder never arrives anywhere: the lift stops with it in mid-air, and the ride is the
mechanism rather than the delivery.
