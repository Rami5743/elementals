# Tandem (`tandem`) — all four

**Introduces:** one switch, two gates and fourteen tiles of corridor — so the team crosses in
waves and the order is the puzzle.

1. **Solution.** Water puts out the flames that shut the near end and takes its gem. Air hops for
   its own gem and into the turbine, which nobody else can reach: both gates swing open for seven
   seconds. Earth, the slowest, takes the first window and runs the corridor, picking up the
   neutral gem in it. Air spins again for Fire, who melts the ice beyond the second gate, again
   for Water, and a fourth time for itself. With everyone through, Earth ground-pounds the cracked
   floor and all four drop into the vault.

2. **Roles.** Water opens the near end, Air opens the corridor as often as it is needed, Fire
   opens the ice at the far end, Earth opens the vault.

3. **Why each is necessary.** Flames, ice and a cracked floor are each one element's business, and
   a turbine answers only to Air. Both gates are three tiles with rock to the roof above them.
   `check` confirms all four counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The windows are not scarce — Air can spin the turbine all day — but each one
   only holds one runner comfortably, so the team has to decide who goes when, and the slowest
   goes first while everyone else is still out of the way.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Being caught between the gates costs nothing: Air spins again
   and the corridor opens. There is no liquid or deadly tile in the level, and the only one-way
   move is the cracked floor at the very end.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The flames, both gates and the ice all run from the floor
  to the roof. The turbine is four tiles up — past Water's and Earth's jumps and inside Air's —
  and seven tiles clear of the first gate so nobody brushes it in passing.
