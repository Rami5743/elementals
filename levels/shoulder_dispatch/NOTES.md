# Shoulder Dispatch

Batch-90: 48/90

**Introduces:** Each of two high turbines needs a head stack, and produces a temporary passage rather than the final route. Turn the temporary passage into a permanent return route before retrieving the near-bank certificate.

## Intended solution
1. Earth stands under the first turbine; Air jumps onto Earth's head.
2. Air jumps again and reaches the first high turbine. Its signal lasts 480 ticks after contact.
3. Earth crosses the temporary gate and operates the far-bank holding lever. The lever opens the west workshop and keeps the gate open.
4. Air collects its gem in the west workshop; Earth returns to operate the near certificate.
5. Earth descends through the newly opened floor shutter; Air follows after Earth reaches the lower turbine.
6. Air uses Earth as the step a second time and gives the opposite-direction pulse.
7. Earth crosses west and operates the second holding lever, enabling the eastern workshop.
8. Earth returns east for the final certificate and gem; both can now cross to their exits in the west.

## Why each player is needed
Air is the only element that spins turbines; Earth provides the missing jump height and crosses the pulse. The high turbines cannot be reached by Air from the floor, and the separate gems require both routes.

## The insight
The partner cannot simultaneously serve as the launch step and run to the far switch. The short pulse must replace their physical presence while they run. A successful crossing converts the transient opening into a reversible stable path for both.

## Motor challenge and recovery
Two timed passages, each with a 480-tick turbine duration. Both have broad flat banks and enough time for a 90-tick delayed departure. That delay was tested for both. Deliberately waiting out each pulse and then stacking and spinning again was also tested successfully. If either player misses a jump, the floor is safe and the turbine can be retried. If a runner is caught beyond the timed gate, the holding lever is on the far side.

## Shortcuts
Unassisted jumps directly beneath the high turbine, a straight route to the exits and skipping the first far holding lever all fail in simulation. Vertical doors extend from floor to ceiling. The floor shutter retains the players upstairs until the near certificate opens it.

## Kinship
Shoulders uses a high turbine as a single permanent key in a four-player map. Here each high turbine provides a transient pulse, followed by a permanent return-path conversion and a second opposite-direction dispatch. Turbine-out-of-reach is new at two players.
