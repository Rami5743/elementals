# Shutter Council

**Introduces:** three-key bridge selection and two serially interdependent certification operators.

## The intended solution
Earth and Air enable the quorum while Fire and Water hand off the bridge selector and travel to their far contacts. Earth relinquishes its enabling key, obtains the first certificate and releases Air's first certification gate. Air relinquishes its key too and obtains the complementary certificate. Both restore the quorum. Fire and Water return for the left relays. Earth then receives permission for its second certificate from the two left contacts and Air's first certificate. Earth's second certificate releases Air's second gate. After Air has certified and both operators have restored their keys, the travelers repeat the bridge handoff to reach the final permission. Water leaves its exit briefly to return Fire home. Earth and Air relinquish the enabling keys one last time and exit.

## Why each player is needed
Fire and Water cross complementary bridges; Earth and Air enable the quorum and perform distinct, serially dependent certifications. Each supplies necessary contacts unavailable to the others.

## The insight
The very keys that enable travel trap their operators. The group must deliberately withdraw and restore the quorum in safe phases. Certificates require contributions from other galleries in a particular order.

## Likely failures and how players recover
Transfer the middle key before releasing it. If a shutter changes prematurely, use the ledges or return to the middle lever. All keys are reversible, with no timed jumps.

## Shortcuts that were closed
Direct walking, omitting the middle handoff, and retaining the lower key during the lower crossing all fail.

## Kinship with the campaign
Extends Quorum Shutters with a second quorum operator and serial, cross-gallery certification, rather than parallel exit corridors.
