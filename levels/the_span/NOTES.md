# The Span (`the_span`) — all four

**Introduces:** a span that exists only while a torch burns — a wide door wired to the torch and
inverted — with the torch on the wrong side of the chasm.

1. **Solution.** Air climbs the four steps to the launch ledge; Fire follows, takes its gem at the
   top and climbs onto Air's head. Air walks off the edge and holds Jump: the pair glide the whole
   chasm, taking the gem on the line, and land on the far bank. Fire walks to the torch and lights
   it, and the span swings shut across the chasm. Water crosses it, collecting the neutral gem on
   the way, and puts out the flames beyond. Earth follows, takes its gem and ground-pounds the
   cracked floor; all four drop into the vault.

2. **Roles.** Air is the only crossing until the span closes. Fire is the only one who can light
   the torch. Water is the only one who can clear the flames. Earth is the only one who can open
   the vault.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The chasm is ten tiles of empty air over sludge, the flames run
   floor to roof, and the vault has no other entrance. `check` confirms all four counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The bridge is wired backwards: it stands open while nothing is happening, so
   the first crossing has to be made in the air, and the only point of carrying a passenger is to
   get the torch lit from the other side.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Leaving the ledge without holding Jump, or stepping off Air
   in mid-air, drops the pair into the sludge and restarts — a few seconds from the spawn. The
   staircase has a one-tile tunnel under it, so the ground route to the span is never blocked.
   Once lit, the torch stays lit and the span is permanent.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The launch ledge is eight tiles above the hall, out of
  reach of a two-player boost, and it stops on the near side of the chasm. The flames reach the
  roof, the torch is four tiles clear of the span's end so nobody brushes it in passing, and the
  far bank's floor is solid everywhere but the three cracked tiles.
