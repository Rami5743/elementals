# Signal Fire (`signal_fire`) — Fire + Water + Air

**Introduces:** a bridge that is only there while a torch burns — a wide door wired to the torch
and inverted, so lighting the torch closes it across the chasm.

1. **Solution.** Water hops for its gem down in the dark. Air climbs the four two-tile steps to
   the launch ledge eight tiles up and stands still; Fire follows, taking its gem on the top step,
   and climbs onto Air's head. Air walks off the edge and holds Jump: the pair glide the whole
   twelve-tile chasm, taking the gem on the line, and land on the far bank. Fire walks to the
   torch and lights it — the bridge swings shut across the chasm — and Water walks over it, puts
   out the flames in front of the doors and all three go home.

2. **Roles.** Air is the only one who can cross an empty chasm. Fire is the only one who can light
   the torch, and so the only cargo worth carrying. Water is the only one who can put out the
   flames, and the one the bridge is for.

3. **Why each is necessary.** There is nothing over the chasm until the torch burns, and the
   sludge at the bottom kills everyone. The flames at the far end are two tiles with rock to the
   roof above them. `check` confirms all three counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The bridge is built backwards: it is open when nothing is happening and closed
   when the torch is lit, so the first crossing has to be made without it — and the only reason to
   carry a passenger over is to get the torch lit from the other side.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** The level is dark, which is most of its difficulty: the
   chasm's edge and the steps have to be read by the glow of the characters until the torch is
   burning. Leaving the ledge without holding Jump, or stepping off Air in mid-air, drops the pair
   into the sludge and restarts — a few seconds from the spawn. Once the torch is lit it stays
   lit, so the bridge is permanent and nothing can be undone.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The chasm is twelve tiles of empty air over sludge, far
  past any jump, and the staircase that reaches the launch ledge stops on the near side. The
  ledge itself is eight tiles up, so a two-player boost from the floor does not reach it either,
  and the tunnel under the steps is one tile high, which no stack of two fits into.
