# Finale (`finale`) — all four

**Brings together:** a turbine only Air can reach, a ford only Water can freeze, a shelf that
takes three players to reach, and a vault only Earth can open.

1. **Solution.** Air hops into the turbine at the near end and the hall latches open; it takes its
   gem four tiles up and waits. Water walks through, takes its gem and stands on the pad: the ford
   freezes over. Air crosses and stands clear. Earth crosses, takes its gem and plants itself
   under the shelf; Water leaves the pad — the ice holds fifteen seconds — and climbs onto Earth,
   and Fire climbs onto Water. Earth jumps, Water a moment later, Fire last, and Fire lands nine
   tiles up on the shelf, taking the neutral gem on the way. Fire melts the ice up there, takes
   its gem and pulls the lever; the gate at the end of the hall opens. Fire drops off the near end
   of the shelf, Earth walks through and ground-pounds the cracked floor, and all four drop into
   the vault.

2. **Roles.** Air opens the hall, Water makes the crossing and is the middle of the tower, Earth
   is the base and opens the vault, Fire rides to the top and works the lever.

3. **Why each is necessary.** A turbine answers only to Air; five tiles of water drown everybody
   but Water; the shelf is nine tiles up, which takes three players jumping in turn; the ice on
   the shelf is Fire's; the vault floor is Earth's. `check` confirms all four counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** Water has to give up the pad to join the tower, which is only safe because the
   ford stays frozen for fifteen seconds after it steps off — the level's last trick is knowing
   that the ice outlasts the player who made it.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Stepping into the ford before it freezes drowns Fire, Earth
   or Air and restarts. The tower's jumps must be staggered — about ten to twenty ticks between
   each pair — and a failed tower costs nothing but a retry. The hall's gate latches, so it is
   never closed again, and the only one-way move is the pound at the very end.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The shelf runs from column 22 into the right-hand wall, so
  there is no way onto it from the far side, and the hall below holds no crate, boulder or ledge
  to climb. Both gates reach the roof, the turbine is five tiles clear of the one it opens, and
  the ford is five tiles wide against a three-tile jump.
