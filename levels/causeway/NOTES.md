# Causeway (`causeway`) — Fire + Water + Earth + Air

## The intended solution

A single tile of open water separates the upper hall from the lower one, and the
corridor over it is one tile high — nobody can jump there at all. Walk into the
gap and you go in the water.

1. **Fire** melts the ice across the hall; **Water** hops over Fire and puts out
   the flames behind it.
2. **Earth** rolls the boulder east and shoves it off the brink. It does not
   sink: a boulder rests on the surface of water, and this one comes to rest with
   its top level with the bank it fell from. The gap is now a floor.
3. **Air** stands on the turbine, which latches the vault in the lower hall open.
4. All four walk over the stone, step down into the lower hall and go to their
   doors.

## Why each player is needed

- **Earth** is the only one who can move a boulder, and the boulder is the only
  thing that will bridge the channel.
- **Air** is the only one who can spin the turbine, and the vault has no other
  key.
- **Fire** melts the ice and **Water** puts out the flames, in series across the
  only corridor; both are three tiles tall under rock.

## The insight

Water is not only something to avoid — it holds things up. A boulder pushed into
the channel floats, and because the bank is exactly one tile above the water, it
floats *flush*: the thing you were treating as a hazard becomes the last plank of
the bridge. The low roof is what makes you look for a floor instead of a jump.

## Likely failures and how players recover

- **Walking into the gap.** You drown (Water does not). Respawn and wait for the
  stone.
- **Pushing the boulder too early.** No harm: it only has one place to go, and
  once it is in the channel it stays there.
- **Trying to jump the gap.** The roof over the crossing is one tile high, so the
  jump never happens. That is the hint.
- **Going back for a gem.** The lower hall is two tiles below the stone, but a
  jump from there reaches it again, so nothing on the far side is a one-way trip.

## Shortcuts that were closed

- The crossing corridor (tiles 17–19) has a one-tile roof, so the gap cannot be
  jumped, and the three-tile part of the hall ends at a rock wall before it, so
  nobody can come in flying.
- The ice and the flames are three tiles tall under rock.
- The vault is three tiles tall under rock in the lower hall.

## Kinship with the campaign

The campaign's boulders are for heavy plates, lifts and slopes (Quarry,
Dumbwaiter, Avalanche). None of them is ever put in the water, and the fact that
a boulder floats is not something any campaign level asks you to notice.
