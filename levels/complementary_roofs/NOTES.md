# Complementary Roofs

Batch-90: 83/90

**Introduces:** Two opposite sliding slabs are both bridges for the upper traveler and ceilings for the lower one. Earth needs each roof removed in turn, while Fire must cross the other bridge and ultimately restore it a third time.

## The intended solution
1. Earth enters the first crawl through its wooden ledge, but the closed roof leaves no headroom for a pound.
2. Fire crosses the intact first bridge and switches the island selector: the first roof retracts and the second bridge closes.
3. Earth pounds the exposed first panel and drops into the first vault. Its certificate unlocks Fire's far workshop.
4. Fire crosses the second bridge and lights the far torch.
5. Earth rides the recall lift to the western bank. Fire returns to the island and reverses the selector, closing the first bridge and retracting the second roof.
6. Earth crosses the first bridge, drops into the second crawl, and pounds its panel. Both earlier certificates are needed for the second vault's gate.
7. Earth reaches the far vault relay and gem, releasing Fire's exit.
8. Fire restores the selector once again to cross the second bridge and walk to the far exit. Earth exits below.

## Why each player is needed
Only Earth can pound either panel and reach the two vault certificates. Only Fire lights the far torch and must remain available at the island selector while Earth is below. Both idle-at-exit checks fail.

## The insight
The same slab is a floor seen from above and a roof seen from below; the two slabs have opposite states. Their removal in the wrong order costs access to a gallery. Fire must undo the earlier choice to deliver Earth to the second crawl and finally undo it once more to exit.

## Likely failures and how players recover
Pounding below a closed slab does not break the floor. The selector is reversible and remains accessible from the island. An interrupted first switch and a closed-roof pound attempt were tested in recovery. Wooden ledges at both crawl mouths allow return; the recall lift returns to the first vault repeatedly. The lower right vault has a one-tile pedestal immediately below the panel to allow return through the hole. No lethal tiles, timed inputs or narrow landing targets. Both panel removals open routes.

## Shortcuts that were closed
Repeated pounding under the closed roof fails. Refusing to reverse the selector keeps the second roof closed and first bridge open. Attempting to cross the second bridge after Earth removes its roof without restoring it fails. All three are scripted.

## Kinship with the campaign
Grand Works asks the party to build and dismantle a route. Crawlspace at four players uses one slab as bridge and roof; here the two opposite slabs share a selector, so alternating their states and restoring the final crossing is the puzzle. The novelty tool flags two shared words in the core tag with Double Deck. That level moves a single lift between two decks, where it provides a crossing at different heights; it has no cracked panels, roof-dependent jump, or opposing slabs whose removal opens a new vault. The physical cause of the interlock is different, and the two-player pound-needs-headroom idea is unused. The original core tag is retained rather than renamed to suppress this warning.
