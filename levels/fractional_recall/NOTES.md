# Fractional Recall

Batch-90: 76/90

**Introduces:** a half-unit rider supplies exactly the missing ballast on a 2:1 rope, maintaining the high contact while Fire leaves to ignite a torch; the third operator needs the complementary low contact twice.

## The intended solution

1. Air boards the ballasted pan, balancing the opposing stone.
2. Fire joins, lowering the pan and raising the stone.
3. Fire dismounts and ignites the remote torch while Air retains the high contact.
4. Air unloads completely, lowering the stone; Water records the first low certificate.
5. Air recalls to the upper floor. Fire follows and both board the pan again.
6. The high contact and Water's certificate open the second workshop; Fire records the second high certificate.
7. Air unloads again, allowing Water to record the second low certificate.
8. Water's certificate releases the three exits. Air uses the recall lift to reach the upper exit.

## Why each player is needed

Fire is uniquely able to ignite the torch. Air's fractional rope weight retains the permission while Fire is away from the pan. Water alone can reach the separate low-contact workshop and its two certificates.

## The insight

The weight needed to move the rope is not the weight needed to hold it. The single comparator must be fully unloaded between its two uses, not merely left in the safe high state.

## Likely failures and how players recover

If Air leaves after Fire enters the first workshop, the gate closes. Fire can light the torch from inside to reopen it. The recovery replay deliberately verifies this closure and then solves. If Fire has not entered yet, both riders can use the independent recall lift again; its dwell is 300 ticks. The shafts have safe floors. No input needs precise timing.

## Shortcuts that were closed

Fire rushing to the torch without the balancing rider and Air remaining on the pan indefinitely both fail in scripted attempts. The doors span their ceilings. The opposing boulder and its heavy contacts are sealed away from all players.

## Kinship with the campaign

Assay compares weights, but here the exact fractional rope weight maintains a useful contact while the ignition-capable rider leaves. Weight of Two at three players compares heavy plates with the rope; this level instead alternates the high and low states through changes of rider load. Adapted from Half-Weight Keeper at four players; fractional-rider-keeper and three-load-rope-control are new to three-player levels.
