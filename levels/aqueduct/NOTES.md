# Aqueduct (`aqueduct`) — Fire + Water + Air

**Introduces:** two machines, each of which strands the player who runs it, arranged so that they
free each other.

1. **Solution.** Water walks left, puts out the flames that shut the near end of the hall, takes
   its gem and stands on the plate behind them: the updraft starts. Meanwhile Fire climbs onto
   Air's head; once the draught is running Air steps into the shaft and the pair drift fourteen
   tiles up, taking the neutral gem on the way, and land in the loft. Fire takes its gem, melts
   the ice that cuts the loft in two and walks to the lever. Water leaves the plate — the updraft
   stops, but nobody needs it now — walks over to the lift and waits on it. Fire pulls the lever,
   the lift brings Water up, and all three walk to their doors.

2. **Roles.** Water is the only one who can reach the plate, and is the one the plate strands.
   Air is the only lift the shaft has. Fire is the only one who can melt the ice and so reach the
   lever that fetches Water.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The flames are Water's alone, and the updraft is far too weak to
   raise Water or Fire by themselves; the lift does not move until the lever is pulled, and the
   lever is behind ice that only Fire melts. `check` confirms all three counterfactuals: whichever
   player waits in its door, the other two stall.

4. **The insight.** Whoever works the plate cannot ride, so the pair who go up owe the one left
   behind a way of following — and that way has to be built from up there.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Nothing in the level is deadly: there is no liquid and
   nothing to fall into except the shaft, which simply drops you back into the hall. Fire slipping
   off Air's head costs a walk back and another climb. Water can step on and off the plate as
   often as it likes, and the lever can be pulled again if the lift is sent away empty.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The loft is fourteen tiles above the hall, four times a
  jump and twice a two-player boost, and the shaft walls are solid rock, so the updraft and the
  lift are the only ways up. The ice is two tiles with rock to the roof above it, and the flames
  likewise, so neither barrier can be jumped. The lift's shaft is capped with a one-way hatch.
