# Opposite Pans

Batch-90: 26/90

**Introduces:** Four votes reverse the imbalance of two fixed cargo loads. The first two exit certificates require the low-left state, whereas the other two require the complementary high-left state, and Air must certify an intermediate reversal before either set can finish.

## The intended solution
1. The four operators engage the ratio. The left stone then outweighs the mechanically advantaged right pan and descends; the right pan rises.
2. Fire, Water and Earth register their first acknowledgments.
3. Fire withdraws A. The right side now outweighs the left. Air crosses its middle gate to record the intermediate acknowledgment.
4. Fire and Earth record the second permissions while Water crosses its live middle gate and records its western permission.
5. Fire restores A. Air's intermediate acknowledgment is required alongside the other inputs for the third workshops; Water is stranded on the west side.
6. Fire and Earth register the third permissions and Water opens the eastern return workshop.
7. Fire withdraws A again. Water returns east to register the fourth permission, then crosses back.
8. Fire engages A again. Fire and Water pass their final gates and register their final certificates.
9. Earth returns to C and withdraws its vote. Both cargo contacts reverse; Earth can now register its own final certificate.
10. The complementary contacts and Earth's final certificate allow Air to leave.

## Why each player is needed
All four votes are required for the ratio, and Air has an independent middle certificate. Fire controls reversible phase changes, Water retrieves the eastern certificate, Earth releases the final vote, and Air finishes after Earth. The four idle-at-exit checks fail.

## The insight
The two heavy cargo loads remain in place on their pans, but mechanical advantage changes the sign of the imbalance. The paired contacts govern the sequence of permissions; one final exit state cannot serve all four players.

## Likely failures and how players recover
Leaving the ratio engaged blocks the second workshops. Return to A and disengage it. Re-engaging prematurely closes Water's live passage; withdrawing A restores it. A simulated recovery deliberately returns to the engaged state after Air's middle certificate, then restores the earlier state, finishing in 60.27 seconds. Intermediate latches retain their certificates and the relevant controls remain reachable. No precision jump or timed input is required. Arbitrary states have not been exhaustively checked.

## Shortcuts that were closed
Walking directly, never reversing the rope, skipping Water's eastern return workshop and retaining all four votes at the final exit fail in simulation. The cargo pans and four galleries are physically separated. All doors reach the ceiling.

## Kinship with the campaign
Counterpoise changes the weight of a load; here the cargo weights are fixed and the ratio reverses the direction of motion. This is the four-player development of Ratio Tribunal. Rope Palindrome, the nearest rope-based four-player level, stores a height in a balanced rope; this puzzle uses intentionally unbalanced states and complementary final certificates.
