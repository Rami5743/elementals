# Deep Shaft (`deep_shaft`) — all four

**Introduces:** a shuttle big enough for all four, whose switch has to be held by a crate because
nobody can stay behind.

1. **Solution.** Fire melts the ice at the near end, takes its gem and shoves the crate from
   behind it onto the plate at the dead end: the shuttle starts running. Air hops into the turbine
   — four tiles up, out of everyone else's reach — and the way to the shuttle latches open. All
   four take berths and ride ten tiles up, Air collecting its gem in the shaft. In the loft Water
   puts out the flames that cut it in two and walks clear; Earth takes its gem, ground-pounds the
   cracked floor and everybody drops into the vault below.

2. **Roles.** Fire opens the dead end and sets the shuttle going, Air opens the way to it, Water
   opens the loft and Earth opens the vault.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The loft is ten tiles above the hall and the shuttle only runs while
   the plate is held — by the crate, because every player has to ride. The ice, the turbine's
   door, the flames and the vault floor are each exactly one element's business. `check` confirms
   all four counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** Four players, four locks, and one machine they all depend on: the crate is
   what turns "somebody has to stay behind" into "nobody has to".

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** The shuttle loops, so a missed boarding costs one pass. The
   crate cannot be lost — the plate is against the west wall. The turbine's door latches, so it
   never needs opening twice. There is no liquid or deadly tile in the level; the only one-way
   step is the vault floor, and the doors are under it.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The shaft's walls are solid rock and the loft is ten tiles
  up, past a jump, a head-stand, a two-player boost and a three-player tower. The ice and the
  turbine's door reach the roof, the turbine is five tiles clear of the door it opens, and the
  loft's floor is solid everywhere but the three cracked tiles.
