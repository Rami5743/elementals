# Letterbox (`letterbox`) — Fire + Water + Earth

## The intended solution

Three tiles of the hall floor are a one-way grating. You can drop through it
yourself by pressing Down, but a boulder standing on it never will: nothing
pushes a boulder through a grating except a door closing on top of it.

1. **Fire** melts the ice at the mouth of the hall; **Water** hops over Fire and
   puts out the flames behind it.
2. Fire and Water walk on past the grating — they have to be on the far side
   before the lid comes down, because the lid fills the hall.
3. **Earth** comes in behind the boulder, rolls it onto the grating directly
   under the lid, and steps back out from under it.
4. **Water** throws the lever. The lid closes on the boulder and presses it
   through the grating, into the cell below and onto the heavy plate. The vault
   latches open.
5. Water throws the lever again so the lid lifts and Earth can come through.
   Everyone walks to their door.

## Why each player is needed

- **Earth** is the only one who can move a boulder, and the plate below is heavy,
  so nothing lighter would hold it.
- **Fire** melts the ice and **Water** puts out the flames; both are three tiles
  tall under rock, in series across the only corridor.
- Someone has to work the lever while Earth is clear of the lid, and that someone
  has to be on the far side of the grating — which is why the order of the first
  three steps matters.

## The insight

The grating looks like a hole you cannot use. It is: you can fall through it, but
the thing you need to get down there cannot. The lid is not a gate in this level,
it is a press — the only machine in the hall that pushes downward.

## Likely failures and how players recover

- **Standing under the lid when it closes.** The door rests on whoever is under
  it; it does not kill you, but it will not close either, and the boulder stays
  where it is. Step aside and throw the lever again.
- **Pushing the boulder past the grating.** Earth can push it back: the hall is
  flat all the way to the vault.
- **Closing the lid with Earth on the near side.** The lever works both ways —
  throw it again.
- **Dropping through the grating yourself.** The cell below has no way out, but a
  respawn costs only the walk back.

## Shortcuts that were closed

- The plate is heavy, so no player can hold it; and a player standing on it is a
  player not standing in a door.
- The ice and the flames are three tiles tall under rock.
- The vault is latched by the plate, so the boulder only has to arrive once.

## Kinship with the campaign

The campaign uses one-way tiles as ledges that keep what a lift leaves on them
(Dumbwaiter). Here the one-way tile is the obstacle rather than the shelf, and
the answer is the one interaction in the game that defeats it.
