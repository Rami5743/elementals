# Lofts (`lofts`) — Fire + Water + Earth + Air

## The intended solution

A single updraft fills the shaft in the middle of the map, at strength 1.5. It
does three different things:

- **Air** it lifts — fifteen tiles, up into the loft at the top.
- **Fire and Water** it does not lift at all. It holds them at the height they
  jumped from and carries them about ten tiles sideways, which is exactly the
  width of the shaft.
- **Earth** barely notices it. Earth jumps in and comes down in the sludge.

1. **Earth** shoves the boulder onto the heavy plate — the vault's first key —
   and stays on the near side, where its own door is.
2. **Air** runs off the lip, lets go of the controls and rides the wind to the
   loft, where it spins the turbine: the second key. The vault latches open.
3. **Fire** jumps into the wind and is carried across to the lower hall, and
   melts the ice there.
4. **Water** follows, hops over Fire, and puts out the flames.
5. Air walks to the end of the loft and drops down the chute into the lower hall,
   past both barriers. Everyone goes to their door.

## Why each player is needed

- **Earth** is the only one who can move the boulder, and the plate is heavy, so
  it is the only one who can hold that key down — with the boulder, because
  Earth has to be standing in its own door at the end.
- **Air** is the only one the wind lifts, so the loft and the turbine are its
  alone.
- **Fire** and **Water** clear the ice and the flames in the lower hall; both are
  three tiles tall under rock.

## The insight

Everyone looks at the same wind and sees a different machine. The second half of
it is Air's: steering is what stops you climbing. Hold right and the wind just
carries you across like Fire. Let go, and it takes you to the top.

## Likely failures and how players recover

- **Earth jumping into the wind.** It gets about three tiles and lands in the
  sludge. There is a checkpoint on the lip for exactly this.
- **Air steering while it rises.** It sails across the shaft instead of up, lands
  in the lower hall, and has no way back to the lip — so it drops in the sludge
  or waits. Respawn at the checkpoint and try again without touching right.
- **Fire or Water jumping too late.** They land short, in the sludge. The run-up
  to the lip is long enough to be certain of it.

## Shortcuts that were closed

- The loft is reachable only through the wind: rows 7–14 above the lower hall are
  solid rock on both sides of the shaft, and the chute down from the loft is one
  tile wide at the far end, four tiles above anyone's jump from below.
- The ice and the flames sit between the landing bank and the vault, so Fire and
  Water cannot skip them. Air lands past them, but Air cannot open the vault on
  its own: the plate is the other key.
- The vault is three tiles tall under rock.

## Kinship with the campaign

Thermals and Chimney Sweep use an updraft to lift somebody, and Thermals makes a
point of Fire being too heavy for one. This level puts all three outcomes in a
single wind at once and makes the difference between them the whole puzzle.
