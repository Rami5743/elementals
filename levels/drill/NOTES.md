# Drill (`drill`) — Fire + Water + Earth + Air

## The intended solution

The right-hand shaft is four tiles wide and sealed at three heights. At each of
those heights only two of the four columns are open, and what fills them is a
cracked floor — one tile further right each time: rows 9 (25–26), 13 (26–27),
17 (27–28). The rest of each of those rows is rock.

1. **Fire** melts the ice across the hall; **Water** hops over it and puts out
   the flames behind.
2. **Air** stands on the turbine, which latches the gate to the shaft head open.
3. **Earth** walks off the lip at tile 24 and drops onto the first hatch. It
   lines up over the cracked tiles, jumps, and pounds. It falls four rows onto
   the second hatch, a tile to the right; lines up, jumps, pounds. Then the
   third. Then the vault floor.
4. With all three hatches gone the shaft is a staircase of holes. The other
   three step off the same lip and steer a tile right at each level on the way
   down. Everybody walks to their door.

## Why each player is needed

- **Earth** is the only one who can pound, and all three hatches are cracked
  floors. Nothing else opens the shaft.
- **Air** is the only one who can spin the turbine, and the gate is the only way
  to the shaft head.
- **Fire** melts the ice and **Water** puts out the flames: both are three tiles
  tall under rock, so neither is jumpable, and they sit in series across the only
  corridor.

## The insight

A ground pound is usually something you do to the floor you are standing on.
Here it is something you do three times in a row, each time from a ledge that is
not above the next one. Lining up matters: Earth is 26 px wide, and if half of
it is resting on the rock beside the hatch, the pound lands on stone and nothing
breaks.

## Likely failures and how players recover

- **Pounding while half on the rock.** Nothing breaks. Step across until you are
  clear of the stone and try again — the hatch is still there.
- **Falling past a hatch on the descent.** You land on the rock shelf beside the
  hole. Walk the one tile across and drop; no hatch is ever more than a tile from
  a standing spot.
- **Dropping into the shaft before Earth has been down.** You land on the first
  hatch and wait there; Earth cannot pound a floor you are standing on without
  taking you with it, which is harmless — you both land on the next one.
- A checkpoint sits in the hall for anyone who wants to start the descent again.

## Shortcuts that were closed

- The three hatch rows are solid rock outside the cracked tiles, so there is no
  straight drop down the shaft and no way past a hatch that is still intact.
- The shaft head is behind a three-tile door under rock, so nobody reaches it
  before Air has spun the turbine.
- The vault has no other entrance: its ceiling is the bottom of the shaft.

## Kinship with the campaign

The campaign uses cracked floors as single trapdoors (Dust Devil, Grand Works and
others). Three of them stacked, offset, and pounded in one descent is a different
shape of problem: the pound stops being a key and becomes a way of travelling.
