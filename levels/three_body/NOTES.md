# Three Body (`three_body`) — Fire + Water + Earth

**Introduces:** the three-player tower. Two players jumping in turn reach about seven tiles; three
reach about eleven, and the shelf is deliberately put at nine.

1. **Solution.** Water hops for its gem and puts out the flames that shut the passage. Earth takes
   its own gem and plants itself under the shelf. Water climbs onto Earth, Fire climbs onto Water.
   Earth jumps, Water jumps a moment later, Fire last — and Fire sails up past the neutral gem
   onto the shelf nine tiles above the floor. Fire melts the ice up there, takes its gem and pulls
   the lever, which opens the gate at the end of the hall. Fire walks off the near end of the
   shelf and drops back into the hall. All three go through the gate; Earth ground-pounds the
   cracked floor of the chamber beyond and everybody drops into the vault, where the three doors
   are.

2. **Roles.** Water opens the passage and is the middle of the tower. Earth is the base and the
   only one who can break the vault floor. Fire is the one thrown, and the only one who can melt
   the ice that guards the lever.

3. **Why each is necessary.** Measured in the game's own simulation, a single jump reaches 2.8
   tiles, a head-stand 3.6, a two-player boost 6.3 and this three-player tower 7.5 tiles above its
   own feet — nine above the floor. Take any one player away and the shelf is unreachable. Beyond
   that each has an irreplaceable job: the flames are Water's, the ice is Fire's and the cracked
   floor is Earth's. `check` confirms all three counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** Height is a team effort, and the order matters: the heaviest, lowest jumper
   goes on the bottom and the one with business upstairs goes on top.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** The jumps have to be staggered, not simultaneous — the
   measured window is wide (anything from about ten to twenty ticks between each pair of presses
   worked) but pressing together wastes the boost. A failed tower drops everyone on open floor
   with nothing lost. There is no liquid in the level and nothing that can be spent, so it cannot
   be made unwinnable; only the cracked floor is one-way, and the doors are on the other side of
   it.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The shelf runs from column 20 into the right-hand wall, so
  there is no way onto it from the far side, and the hall below has no ledge, crate or boulder to
  climb. The flames and the gate are both barriers with rock above them to the roof, and the ice
  on the shelf likewise. Nothing but a ground pound opens the vault.
