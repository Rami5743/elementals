# Draught — notes

**Brings together:** a boulder that is the only weight that will hold a draught open, a loft
gate wired against that same weight, and a hatch that only a torch at the top of the shaft
opens — so the three of them have to take turns being stuck.

## The intended solution

Lower hall, left to right: Fire, Air and Earth, the earth gem, the boulder at 8, the shaft
(columns 16–18) and, past it, the heavy plate at 27–28 and the hatch at 30 that stands
between Earth and its exit.

Wiring: `weigh` (heavy, boulder-only in practice) drives `updraft` — the shaft wind — and
also `gate`, the loft exit gate at 27, **inverted**. `beacon`, the torch on the loft, drives
`hatch`.

1. Fire climbs onto Air's head in the left hall. Both wait there: the whole hall between 8
   and 28 is the boulder's lane.
2. Earth takes the earth gem and pushes the boulder the length of the hall onto the plate.
   The draught starts. At that instant the loft gate shuts.
3. Air walks into the shaft with Fire aboard, rides it up past the neutral gem, steers out
   onto the loft and sets Fire down.
4. Fire lights the torch. The hatch opens — Earth can now reach its exit, but will not yet.
5. Fire and Air take the two loft gems and come to the gate at 27. It is shut, and will stay
   shut while the plate is held.
6. Earth hops over the boulder, pushes it back off the plate. The draught dies — nobody needs
   it any more — and the loft gate opens.
7. All three walk out.

## Roles

- **Earth** — the only one who can move the boulder, and so the only one who can both start
  and stop the draught. It is also the only one the hatch keeps in.
- **Air** — the only one the draught lifts, and the only way Fire reaches the loft.
- **Fire** — the only one who can light the torch, and the torch is the only thing that opens
  the hatch.

## Why each is necessary

No Earth: no weight on the plate, no draught, nobody reaches the loft. No Air: Fire never gets
up the shaft, the torch stays dark, Earth stays behind the hatch. No Fire: the hatch never
opens, and taking the boulder off the plate strands Earth for good.

## The insight

The plate is wired two ways at once. The same weight that makes the climb possible is what
holds the climbers in, so the boulder has to be put on *and* taken off, in that order, with
the torch lit in between — and the one who can take it off is the one who cannot get out
until someone else has been to the top.

## Likely failures and recovery

- **Taking the boulder off before the torch is lit.** The draught dies, and with it the climb.
  Earth can push it back on; nothing is lost but time.
- **Earth going to the hatch first.** The hatch is shut until the torch burns, so there is
  nothing to do over there; walking back costs the hall twice.
- **Fire and Air standing in the hall.** The boulder cannot be pushed past a player, and the
  lane is seventeen tiles long. They wait at the left end, where it starts.
- **Waiting at the loft gate for it to open by itself.** It is wired against the plate; the
  message has to go down to Earth.
