# Beacon Chain

Batch-90: 53/90

**Introduces:** Two permanent beacons create bridges in different galleries. Access to the second beacon depends on successful use of the first.

## Intended solution
1. Air authorizes Fire's first beacon.
2. Fire goes east and lights the first torch, closing Water's bridge over the gap.
3. Water crosses the completed bridge and grants Fire access to the west beacon.
4. Fire returns west and lights the second torch, creating Earth's bridge.
5. Earth crosses west and issues the certificate that, together with the second beacon, releases Air's east workshop.
6. Air goes east and releases Water's west workshop. Water returns west and issues Earth's return authorization.
7. Earth returns east and issues the final Fire and Air permissions.
8. Fire returns east, gets its gem and gives the last joint certificate; Air returns west and unlocks Water's and Earth's exits.
9. All four exit.

## Why each player is needed
Only Fire lights the torches. Water has the first bridge and the second beacon's authorization. Earth has the second bridge and the reciprocal permission. Air authorizes the initial and final routes and connects the two travelers.

## The insight
A permanent bridge is constructed by a signal that normally would open a door: inversion turns the closed door into a floor. The second construction is accessible only after the first is used, so the two permanent signals cannot be issued at once.

## Likely failures and recovery
Do not enter a gap before the torch is lit and the bridge has fully closed. Wait safely on the bank. Lighting a torch is permanent and never destroys an earlier route; both bridges remain available for return travel. Returning toward the second beacon after an unnecessary departure is safe and was simulated.

## Shortcuts
Early attempts at both crossings and skipping the second beacon fail. The eleven-tile gaps are wider than Water's and Earth's jumps; the other galleries are solidly isolated.

## Kinship
Signal Fire makes one torch-built bridge at three players. This puzzle constructs two bridges in a forced authorization order and requires opposite return journeys. Torch-bridge is new at four players.
