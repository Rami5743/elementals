# Travelling Jack

Batch-90: 62/90

## Core idea
Two lifts can both press a heavy object into high contacts, but the same stone is needed at both. The first press must be replaced by two permanent permissions before it can be dismantled and the stone taken to the second shaft. The first heavy contact and the lower heavy contact are required together, so Earth cannot impersonate the stone.

## Solution
1. Fire rides the first jack to the left loft and sets the upper certificate.
2. Air lowers the jack. Earth pushes the stone onto the first berth and retreats.
3. Air raises the stone into the high heavy plate. The press and Fire's certificate open the first lower crossing.
4. Earth occupies the lower heavy contact while the stone holds the high one, latching the certificate compartment.
5. Earth records two permissions which replace the first press, allowing Air into the second control station.
6. Air carries Water to the right loft; Water records the upper certificate. Air returns the second jack to its loading berth.
7. Air returns to lower the first jack. Earth gets to the west side of the stone and moves it east onto the second jack.
8. Air presses the same stone into the second high contact. Earth records the final permission and the four players reach their exits.

## Recovery
Both jacks are reversible, their lower berths have one-way floors so a missing platform cannot drop cargo into a pit, and all access certificates except the deliberately replaceable first crossing latch or are permanent levers. A replay repeats the first press after both jacks are lowered, then lowers it again and finishes. No timed input is needed. The stone cannot leave the lower hall except on a jack, and Earth can always approach it from either side by jumping over it in the three-tile hall. 

## Novelty
Lift-press is new at four players. Jackshaft uses a single jack that is spent permanently at the end. Here the first press is deliberately temporary and must be replaced by a pair of permanent certificates; the same physical stone is then reused at a second jack, after two distinct loft deliveries. The two simultaneous heavy contacts rule out substituting Earth's body for the first load.
