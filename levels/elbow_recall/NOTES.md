# Elbow Recall

Batch-90: 88/90

**Introduces:** lift-corner-path at four players. A corner shuttle is both the bridge across a pit and the only elevator to the far gallery; two geographically separated controls must power successive trips with an intervening lower receipt.

## Solution
Fire melts the ice, Earth pushes the boulder onto the heavy plate and Fire and Water board the shuttle at its low berth. Air activates the first permit; the boulder's contact and the permit together move the shuttle horizontally, then vertically. Water extinguishes the far firewall and pulls the first receipt. Fire and Water reach their exits. Air must revoke the first permit to recall the shuttle and unblock the upper corridor. The first receipt unlocks Earth's lower certificate, which Earth reaches past the heavy plate; on the way back Earth jumps over the stone to avoid dislodging it. The lower certificate unlocks Air's second control. Earth boards at home; Air dispatches from the second control and Earth rides to the upper gallery, issues the upper certificate and reaches the exit. Air's exit now opens.

## Necessity, shortcuts and recovery
Fire alone melts the ice; Earth alone parks the boulder and boards after issuing the lower receipt; Water alone clears the far flames; Air alone operates both controls. Omitting either dispatch or retaining the first permit fails in simulation. The shuttle stops indefinitely at either end and returns to home upon revocation. A premature dispatch reversal with riders aboard was simulated: return to home, then reissue the first permit. There is no timing requirement. Jump over, not into, the stone when leaving the western receipt alcove.

## Novelty
The Crossing at three players supplied the corner path, but its loop runs continuously after the stone is parked. Here the route has two stationary endpoints, requires a second operator and must be deliberately recalled and redispatched after certificates on different storeys. The lower certificate cannot be reached before the first upper inspection. No four-player level has used lift-corner-path.
