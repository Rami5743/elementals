# Caryatid — resume notes

**Design in two sentences.** A lift comes down only while something heavy is held at shoulder
height in its shaft (a heavy call plate hangs there), and since it never crushes it settles on that
load: Earth standing on the stone calls it for the first crate, but the second crate's niche is
behind a door only Earth can hold from the counterweight room, so Water has to jump up under the
stone on the grating and carry it on its head instead. Fire rides the lift down both times and
pushes each crate off into its niche; the exit gate opens when both niche plates are pressed.
