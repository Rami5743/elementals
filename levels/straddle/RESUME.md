# Straddle — resume notes

**Design (two sentences).** A plate senses any body overlapping it, so one body on the join of two
adjacent plates presses both: door A wants four presses from three pairs (the stone starts across
the crawl pair as two of them), and the latched exit wants the stone dropped into its slot plus
Earth on the heavy plate back home. Spending the stone takes two of A's presses away, so Earth's
trip home and everybody's way out are paid for with seams: Fire on the home pair, Water on the
crawl pair the stone left, and Earth on the heavy|light hall pair, which only Earth counts twice.
