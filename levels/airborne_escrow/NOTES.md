# Airborne Escrow

Batch-90: 87/90

**Introduces:** rope-keeps-the-jumper at three players, with independent revocable permission to energize the draught and a return receipt that requires the operator to revoke it.

## Solution
Fire goes east along the isolated upper passage and activates the fan permit. Earth holds the first heavy bellows. Water rides the draught up, boards the loaded pan and rides it down, opening the upper gate and lower hatch. Earth crosses to the second bellows while Water stays on the pan. Water jumps straight into the wind: the rope still counts the airborne rider, so the boulder holds the upper gate open until Water lands beyond it. Once the rope unloads, the complementary inner gate opens. Water issues the first receipt. Fire returns to the east permit and switches it off, then goes west through the now-open interlock and issues the release. Earth reaches the final contact and Water issues the far receipt before returning to its exit; their joint authorization opens Fire's far exit.

## Necessity, shortcuts and recovery
Earth alone holds the heavy bellows; Water must ride the rope and leave it directly into the draught. Fire alone can issue and revoke the separate fan permit. Six shortcuts fail: missing/retaining the fan permit, bypassing the pan, leaving before Earth crosses, landing before flying straight at the gate, or Water trying to substitute for Earth on the bellows. A missed jump that lands on the pan can be retried. If Water lands on the hall floor, the second bellows remains active; climb back into the pan from the upper ledge, board to the right of the stone, wait for the pan to bottom, and jump directly into the wind again. Both recovery routes were simulated, as was a 90-tick delayed departure. There are no lethal tiles.

## Novelty
Ballast at two players supplied the airborne load-retention mechanic. Here the third player holds an independent permission that must persist through both of Earth's bellows positions, but must be withdrawn after the landing receipt. The three-player catalogue has not used rope-keeps-the-jumper. Queue Dispatcher shares revocable permit terminology, but its physics is an exchange of leading and following travelers in a single-file throat with a return fan. Here the core is continuous retention of a pulley load while airborne, required to hold the very gate through which Water is flying; there is no queue or traveler order exchange. Its permit additionally powers the flight itself, not a throat. This is a different mechanism, not a renamed dispatch puzzle.
