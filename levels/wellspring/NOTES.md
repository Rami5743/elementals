# Wellspring (`wellspring`) — Water + Earth

**Introduces:** a shuttle lift on a heavy plate, and a freeze pad worked from above to open a
route down below.

1. **Solution.** Water walks onto the parked shuttle; Earth steps onto the heavy plate and the
   shuttle starts running. Water rides nine tiles up to the gallery, steps off and stands on the
   freeze pad, which turns the pool down in the hall to ice. Earth leaves the plate, walks across
   the frozen pool — taking the neutral gem in it and its own gem in the mud beyond — hops over the
   boulder at the far wall and pushes it all the way back along the hall onto the plate. With the
   boulder holding the plate the shuttle keeps running, so Earth hops back over the boulder,
   waits for the shuttle at the bottom and rides up. Water takes its gem and both walk to their
   doors.

2. **Roles.** Earth is the weight, twice: first its own on the plate, then the boulder's. Water is
   the passenger and the one who makes the hall crossable.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The plate is heavy, so Water on it does nothing and the shuttle
   never runs for Water alone. The pool is five tiles of water, well past Earth's two-tile liquid
   crossing, and only Water on the pad freezes it — so Earth never reaches the boulder without
   Water, and Water never reaches the gallery without Earth. `check` confirms both.

4. **The insight.** The player who holds the plate cannot be the player who rides, so the plate
   has to end up held by something that is not a player — and the only such thing in the level is
   on the wrong side of a pool that the rider has to freeze from above.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Water must stay on the pad while Earth is on the ice: the
   pool stays frozen for ten seconds after Water steps off, which is slack enough to be safe but
   not an invitation to wander. Stepping into unfrozen water drowns Earth and restarts. The
   shuttle loops, so a missed boarding simply means waiting for the next pass. The boulder cannot
   be lost: pushed past the plate it sits on open floor and can be pushed back.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The gallery is nine tiles above the hall floor, out of
  reach of a jump, a head-stand or a two-player boost, and the lift shaft is capped with a one-way
  hatch so nothing can be dropped down it. The boulder is against the hall's right wall, where
  Earth has to hop over it to get behind it, and nothing but Earth can move it at all.
