# The Drop (`the_drop`) — Water + Earth + Air

**Introduces:** a button that opens two things — the gate the others have to run, and the floor the
presser is standing on. Pressing it is not a favour somebody does from safety; it is how that
player leaves the upper hall, and it starts everyone's clock at the same moment.

1. **Solution.** Water and Earth take their gems and stand at the gate at the end of the upper
   hall. Air takes its own gem four tiles up — nothing else gets near it — and places itself under
   the button, which is also four tiles up and equally out of everyone else's reach. Air jumps
   into the button: the gate swings open for five seconds and the trapdoor under Air opens for
   good. Air falls into the lower hall; Water and Earth run the length of the upper hall, through
   the gate and down the shaft beyond it. Below, Water puts out the flames in front of the doors
   and Earth rolls the boulder onto the heavy plate, which opens the last gate.

2. **Roles.** Air is the only one who can reach the button, and the only one who can afford to:
   the floor goes with it. Water clears the flames, Earth is the only mass the plate accepts.

3. **Why each is necessary.** The button is at 288–320 pixels and Air's jump puts its body at
   300–328; Water's reaches 332 and Earth's 364, so neither can touch it. The flames and the last
   gate run from the floor to the roof. `check` confirms all three counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** The press costs the presser their footing, so the order is forced: everybody
   else has to be standing at the gate before Air jumps, because nobody can open it a second time
   from down there.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Missing the window shuts Water or Earth in the upper hall
   with the trapdoor already open — and the trapdoor is the way down, so they drop through it and
   join Air below. Nothing is lost and nothing is deadly; the boulder ends against the west wall
   on its plate. The five seconds are generous for the eight-tile run, which is the point: the
   difficulty is being in position, not being fast.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The gate and the flames both reach the roof. The button's
  only inputs are its own two outputs, and it is ten tiles from the gate, so nobody brushes it
  while running. The trapdoor is the only hole in the upper floor besides the shaft beyond the
  gate, and neither can be reached from below.

- **Kinship with the campaign.** Timed buttons and runs appear in the campaign (l60, l69), and the
  kit's own notes mention putting a trapdoor under a button so that it cannot be pressed twice.
  Here that trapdoor is the point rather than the precaution: the press is also the descent.
