# Windlass (`windlass`) — Earth + Air

**Introduces:** a turbine driving two gates at once, so the corridor between them has to be run.

1. **Solution.** Earth walks up to the first gate and waits there. Air hops into the turbine at the
   far left: both gates swing open and stay open for six seconds. Earth sets off at once, wading
   the mud for its gem, hopping for the neutral gem and running the last stretch through the second
   gate. Air follows, clearing the mud with a running jump — mud kills Air — hopping for its own
   gem over the corridor and stopping short of the plate. Earth jumps over the boulder at the far
   wall and shoves it back onto the heavy plate; the last gate opens and Earth walks to its door.
   Air hops over the boulder and takes the other.

2. **Roles.** Air is the only one who can start the clock, and the only one fast enough to spin the
   turbine and still beat it. Earth is the only one who can move the boulder, and the only one the
   mud is safe for.

3. **Why each is necessary.** Nothing but Air spins a turbine, and both gates are three tiles with
   rock above them to the roof. The last plate is heavy, so Air on it does nothing and the boulder
   is the only mass in the level — and only Earth pushes a boulder. `check` confirms both
   counterfactuals.

4. **The insight.** One switch, two doors, and the distance between them is the puzzle. Standing
   at the first gate before the turbine is spun is worth more than running fast afterwards.

5. **Likely failures and recovery.** Missing the window shuts you in the corridor between the
   gates; Air walks back to the turbine and spins it again, so it costs time and nothing else. The
   mud is deadly to Air and safe for Earth, which is the one hazard in the level and is three
   tiles wide in plain sight. The boulder cannot be lost: the wall behind it stops it, and pushed
   too far left it sits on open floor where Earth can push it back.

- **Shortcuts considered and blocked.** The turbine is seven tiles clear of the first gate, so
  nobody can brush it while squeezing through, and six tiles of corridor separate the mud from the
  second gate. The boulder stands against the end wall, so Earth has to jump over it to get
  behind it, and Air cannot push it at all. Air's extra jump height buys it the gem over the
  corridor and nothing else: the gates run floor to roof.
