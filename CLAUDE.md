## This repository

Thirty finished levels of our own live in `levels/`, published to `index.html` and GitHub Pages.
Work on `main`.

- **Before designing anything, read `catalog/LEVELS.md`** — every level in this repo and all 75
  campaign levels, with its one-line idea and its feature inventory. `catalog/MAPS.md` has the maps
  of this repo's levels. Both are generated by `node tools/catalog.mjs`.
- A new level must differ materially from the other levels **for the same player count**; levels for
  different player counts may share ideas. `node tools/novelty.mjs <id>` measures it,
  `node tools/roll-features.mjs <n>` suggests ingredients that this player count has not worn out.
- The **`new-level` skill** (`.claude/skills/new-level/`) is the full procedure, including the
  measured behaviour of the simulation in `references/engine-facts.md` — read that before writing
  tiles or a solution script; most of it is not guessable.
- After `kit.mjs check` passes, run `node tools/build.mjs` **and** `node tools/catalog.mjs`, or the
  level is invisible to the page and to the next novelty check.
