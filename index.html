Elementals Levels

Co-op puzzle levels for Elementals, made with the Level Kit. Every level has been run against the game's own simulation: it is solvable, every gem is reachable within par, and no player is a passenger.

  1. Open a level's Play link. The game loads with the level added to My Levels in that browser.
  2. Pick the group of elements the level is made for, and have each player press Interact on their controls.
  3. Links accumulate: open several and they all stay in My Levels.
